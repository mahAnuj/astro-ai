# The objective

> **Move the 6–20 position band into the top 5 on the ten highest-impression queries.**

Six teams run on separate schedules and cannot see each other's work. This is the one line they all
rank against. `chief` owns it and rewrites it at most once a quarter, only from the analyst's
measured data.

Set from **#267** — *"Organic traction is real and unpromoted — optimise the 6–20 band before
publishing more."* Queries sitting at positions 6–20 are one or two places from page-one traffic.
They need no new content, which is why they outrank writing more of it.

**North star:** organic sessions. **This quarter's lever:** the band. Monetisation comes later and
is not an agent's decision; the metrics that serve it — sessions, pages per session, returning
users — are the same ones here.

---

## What each team contributes, and how it is scored

A team's output is judged on its contribution to the objective, not on its own volume. Issues
filed, articles drafted and PRs merged are activity, not progress.

| Team | Contributes | Scored on |
|---|---|---|
| **Analytics** | Finds the band, scores every ship against its thesis | Were the scores posted, and were the misses reported as plainly as the hits |
| **Growth** | Picks which band queries are winnable and specifies the work | Did the queries it chose actually move |
| **Engineering** | Ships it without breaking what already ranks | Merged work that moved its metric, and no regression on a ranking page |
| **Design** | Makes the ranked page worth staying on | CTR at unchanged position; funnel step-through |
| **Research** | Makes every claim on those pages true and sourced | Unsourced claims remaining on user-facing pages |
| **Newsroom** | Publishes the receipts on anujmahajan.com | Posts that carry a measured number |

---

## What no team may optimise for

Six loops each maximising their own output will pull the site apart. These are the local optima to
refuse:

- **Analytics** — do not report only what moved. A ship that did nothing is the most useful finding
  of the cycle, and suppressing it makes the whole loop decorative.
- **Growth** — do not file more issues to look productive. The cap is 30 a cycle and the real
  constraint is how many carry a complete Evidence Block. An issue without one is not work.
- **Engineering** — do not ship volume. A merged PR that moved no metric and broke nothing is a
  neutral outcome, not a good one.
- **Design** — do not redesign a page that already ranks without a CTR or funnel number to beat.
  #244 is flagged *"highest silent-breakage risk in the epic"* for a reason.
- **Research** — do not stop at finding problems. An `UNSOURCED` finding with no proposed
  qualification is half a result.
- **Newsroom** — do not publish on a schedule. No measured result, no post.

---

## When the objective and the truth collide

They will. #177/#178 say the 36-point Ashtakoota engine is untraceable; #209 says `/kundli` and
`/about` advertise three things the engine does not compute. Those pages earn traffic.

**Authenticity wins on the claim; traffic wins on the roadmap.** A shipped claim that cannot be
sourced is corrected or qualified — never left standing because the page performs. Under
`SOURCES.md` the defect is *the silence, not the value*, so declaring which school a reading
follows is usually the whole fix, and it is cheap. Removing a working feature is the last resort,
not the first.

What to build **next** is ranked on traffic. What is already shipped is ranked on truth.

---

## Reviewing it

`/sync` checks progress against this file. `chief` rewrites it only when the analyst shows the band
has moved or the lever has stopped working. An objective that changes with the weather is not an
objective, and four loops re-pointed mid-quarter lose everything they had in flight.
