# Aspects (Drishti) — How Planets Influence Each Other

Vedic aspects — called **Drishti** (literally "sight") — are the
mechanism by which a planet in one part of the chart influences
another part. Along with sign lordship and house occupancy, aspects
are one of the three primary tools for reading a Vedic chart.

**Primary sources referenced:**
- BV Raman, *Astrology for Beginners*, Ch. II, pp. 13-15 (henceforth **Raman**)
- Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra (**BPHS**) — the canonical source
- Phaladeepika (**Phala**) — for temporal quality rules
- Cross-links: houses covered in `houses.md`; yogas in
  `vedic_reference.md §4`; Manglik (Mars 7th aspect) in `§1`.

## The two key differences from Western aspects

If you're coming from Western astrology, read this section first — the
underlying model is different.

**1. Sign-to-sign, not degree-to-degree.**
Vedic aspects are counted **from sign to sign**, not from exact degrees
with orbs. [Raman, Ch. II, p. 13] — "According to Hindu Astrology,
aspects are counted from sign to sign." A planet in Cancer aspects
another planet in a specific sign; the exact degrees inside those
signs don't change whether the aspect exists.

**2. Aspects are one-way.**
Planet A aspects planet B doesn't automatically mean B aspects A.
Aspects flow *outward* from the aspecting planet. The receiver may
or may not aspect back, depending on which special aspect (if any)
their own planet carries.

## The universal aspect: opposition (7th house)

**Every planet aspects the sign directly opposite itself with full
sight.** [Raman, Ch. II, p. 13]

- Sun in Cancer → aspects Capricorn (7th from Cancer) with full sight.
- Moon in Aries → aspects Libra (7th from Aries) with full sight.

This is the classical **opposition aspect** (Sapta Drishti). It's the
one aspect every planet shares.

**Reading the opposition:**
- **Benefics aspecting each other** = mutual strengthening. [Raman] —
  "The opposition (7th house) aspect becomes extremely good when it is
  produced by Jupiter and the Moon. It is held to be good when
  benefics aspect each other."
- **Malefic aspecting benefic across 7th** = tension, obstruction
  of the benefic's karaka theme.
- **Planet aspecting its own house across 7th** = strengthens that
  house's significations. e.g. Venus in the 1st aspecting the 7th
  (where Venus rules Libra) → "a good and fair-looking wife and
  happiness on that account." [Raman]

## The three special aspects (Vishesh Drishti)

Three planets have **additional** aspects beyond the universal 7th.
This is unique to Vedic astrology (Western tradition doesn't recognise
these).

| Planet | Special aspects | Nature of the extra sight |
|--------|----------------|--------------------------|
| **Mars** | 4th house + 8th house | Confrontational — Mars projects will |
| **Jupiter** | 5th house + 9th house | Benefic — Jupiter projects grace |
| **Saturn** | 3rd house + 10th house | Restrictive — Saturn projects discipline |

Everything else (Sun, Moon, Mercury, Venus, Rahu, Ketu) has *only* the
7th house aspect.

### Mars aspects — 4th, 7th, 8th

- **Mars aspects the 4th house from itself** — often called the
  "square" aspect in Western terms. [Raman, Ch. II, p. 15]
- **Mars aspects the 7th house from itself** — the universal opposition.
- **Mars aspects the 8th house from itself.**
- **This is the source of Manglik Dosha** — Mars in the 1st, 4th, 7th,
  or 8th (or 2nd and 12th in extended tradition) creates the classical
  affliction because Mars aspects the 7th house (spouse) from all
  those positions. See `vedic_reference.md §1` for the full Manglik
  rules including cancellations.

**When Mars is a Yogakaraka** — Cancer and Leo ascendants — its
special aspects become *beneficial*. [Raman, Ch. II, p. 15] — "If
Mars is the lord of the 4th and the 9th houses, he gets special
qualifications to produce good aspects (by the 4th house aspect).
The significations of the aspected planet and the house are greatly
enhanced."

### Jupiter aspects — 5th, 7th, 9th

- **Jupiter aspects the 5th house from itself** — Western trine.
- **Jupiter aspects the 7th house from itself** — the universal.
- **Jupiter aspects the 9th house from itself** — Western trine.

Because 5th and 9th are trines (always benefic houses), Jupiter's
aspect on any planet or house is **almost always beneficial**.

[Raman, Ch. II, p. 15] — "Jupiter's aspect over any planet, as a
natural benefic, is of weighty importance, apart from the fact that
he may be temporarily ill-disposed."

**Practical rule:** if a planet or house is receiving Jupiter's aspect
(5th, 7th, or 9th), it is significantly protected — even if that
planet is otherwise afflicted. This is why "Guru drishti" (Jupiter's
gaze) is one of the most-sought reliefs in classical remedies.

### Saturn aspects — 3rd, 7th, 10th

- **Saturn aspects the 3rd house from itself.**
- **Saturn aspects the 7th house from itself** — the universal.
- **Saturn aspects the 10th house from itself.**

[Raman, Ch. II, p. 15] — "Saturn aspects the 3rd house and the 10th
house. As a natural malefic his aspects are bad. But they produce
good in case Saturn acquires beneficence by temporal situations. The
opposition aspect has the same power to do good or bad as
conjunction, according to the nature of the planet involved."

**Practical rule:** Saturn's 3rd aspect is heavier than it looks —
it's the aspect responsible for a lot of "career hits at the wrong
time" patterns because Saturn frequently ends up aspecting the 10th
house of profession (Saturn in 8th aspects 10th; Saturn in 12th
aspects 3rd + 10th; Saturn in 1st aspects 3rd + 7th + 10th).

## Aspect strength (Chatushpada Drishti — the four-fold theory)

Classical BPHS gives partial strengths for aspects other than the
universal 7th. This is what Raman calls the four sub-aspects "with a
quarter sight / half sight / three-quarters sight / full sight":

| From aspecting planet's sign | Strength |
|------------------------------|----------|
| 3rd and 10th sign | ¼ sight (25%) |
| 5th and 9th sign | ½ sight (50%) |
| 4th and 8th sign | ¾ sight (75%) |
| 7th sign | Full sight (100%) |

**But** — the special aspects of Mars (4th, 8th), Jupiter (5th, 9th),
and Saturn (3rd, 10th) are always considered **full-strength** for
those three planets regardless of the general table. That's what
makes them "special" — they override the partial-strength default.

In practice most Vedic astrologers treat aspects as binary — the
aspect either exists (special or 7th) or it doesn't — and use the
partial-strength theory only for advanced Shadbala (six-fold
strength) calculations. See `vedic_reference.md §Shadbalas` and
Raman Ch. III p. 26 for the full Drukbala scoring.

## Reading aspects: the practical framework

Given a planet in a chart, walk this sequence:

1. **Which sign is the planet in?** (e.g. Mars in Aquarius)
2. **What is the 7th sign from that?** (e.g. Leo — universal opposition)
3. **If Mars, also check the 4th and 8th signs** (Taurus and Virgo)
4. **What planets or house-lords sit in those aspected signs?** — those
   are the aspected planets.
5. **What houses do the aspected signs occupy in this chart** (from
   the Ascendant)? — those are the aspected houses.
6. **Is the aspecting planet a benefic or malefic** for THIS chart
   (temporal, not natural)?
7. **Read the interaction:** benefic + benefic aspects = doubly good;
   benefic + malefic = mixed with the aspecting planet's flavour
   winning; malefic + malefic = double affliction unless one cancels
   the other via own-sign or exaltation.

## Aspects between planets vs aspects on houses

Vedic tradition uses aspects both ways:

**Aspect on a planet:** Mars aspects Venus → colours Venus's
significations (relationships, comfort, art) with Mars's flavour
(aggression, drive, heat). Common reading: "aggressive pursuit in
love matters."

**Aspect on a house (empty or occupied):** Jupiter aspects the 7th
house even without any planet there → the significations of the 7th
(spouse, partnerships, foreign travel) receive Jupiter's grace.
Common reading: "wise or dharmic spouse; long-lasting business
partnerships; travel that expands one's mind."

Both readings are legitimate. When both apply — Jupiter aspects the
7th house AND the planet Venus sits in the 7th — the reading
combines: "spouse embodies Venus + Jupiter qualities" (refined,
educated, cultured, wise).

## Common aspect combinations worth memorising

These come up in nearly every chart and are worth having pre-loaded:

- **Jupiter aspecting Moon** (from 5th, 7th, or 9th from Moon) = "Gaja
  Kesari Yoga precursor" — see `vedic_reference.md §4.3`. Peace of
  mind, ethical bearing, respected teacher figure.
- **Saturn aspecting Sun** = tension between father and self;
  authority struggles; potentially the "hard-earned respect" archetype.
  Also creates delays in early career.
- **Mars aspecting Venus** = passionate but volatile relationships;
  strong physical/sensual drive; art with edge.
- **Rahu conjunct/aspecting Sun** = eclipse-like effect on soul-Sun
  themes; identity confusion or ambition to disrupt existing power
  structures.
- **Jupiter aspecting 7th house** = one of the classical strongest
  marriage-blessing patterns. Often over-rides Manglik.
- **Saturn aspecting 5th house** = delayed or fewer children; deep
  purva-punya work.
- **Mars aspecting 7th house** (Mars in 1st, 4th, 7th, 10th from 7th
  = Mars in 7th, 4th, 1st, 10th of natal) = the Manglik Dosha vector.
  See `vedic_reference.md §1`.

## How to phrase aspects in a reading

- **Don't lead with the technical name.** Not "Jupiter's 5th aspect on
  your 11th house" (jargon salad) — instead "your gains house receives
  Jupiter's blessing, which classically means…" (concrete meaning).
- **When a benefic aspects an afflicted house, name it as protection.**
  This is one of the most reassuring things you can honestly say. "Yes,
  Mars sits in a rough spot — but Jupiter's aspect softens the impact."
- **When a malefic aspects a benefic karaka, don't catastrophise.** Say
  "the [karaka significations] play out with more friction than the
  average person experiences" — not "expect disaster."
- **Multiple aspects on the same house = multiple flavours, not multiple
  intensities.** Don't stack "afflictions" — a house receiving Mars +
  Saturn is complex, not doubly-doomed.
