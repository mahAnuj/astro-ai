# The 12 Bhavas (Houses) — What Each One Rules

Source-anchored reference for the twelve houses of a Vedic chart.

**Primary sources referenced in this file:**
- BV Raman, *Astrology for Beginners*, UBSPD (henceforth **Raman**)
- Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra (**BPHS**) — chapter/verse where quoted
- Phaladeepika (**Phala**) — for classical short-form rules
- Cross-links to sibling docs use `see vedic_reference.md §N`

**Scope:** every house has ~10-40 significations across many classical
texts. This file captures the **most-cited, most-actionable** ones —
the ones a chart engine or LLM prompt actually needs to produce
grounded interpretations. Rarer significations are noted only when
they change how a house is judged in a common scenario.

---

## Classification of houses (essential context)

Every classical rule about houses depends on where the house sits
relative to the Ascendant.

| Group | Houses | Nature |
|-------|--------|--------|
| **Kendra** (angles) | 1, 4, 7, 10 | Pillars of the chart — strongest houses; planets here get power |
| **Trikona** (trines) | 1, 5, 9 | Houses of dharma & fortune — always benefic |
| **Trik / Dusthana** (evil) | 6, 8, 12 | Houses of struggle — planets here often afflicted |
| **Panapara** (succeedent) | 2, 5, 8, 11 | Support houses — money, resources, gain |
| **Apoklima** (cadent) | 3, 6, 9, 12 | Movement houses — action, service, journeys |
| **Upachaya** (growing) | 3, 6, 10, 11 | Houses that improve with time — malefics here become useful |
| **Maraka** (death-giving) | 2, 7 | Houses whose lords can inflict death when timed by dasa |

Rules that follow apply constantly:

- **Lords of trines (1, 5, 9) always produce good.** [Raman, Ch. III, p. 29]
- **Lords of quadrants (1, 4, 7, 10) become malefic when they are natural benefics** (Jupiter, Venus, waxing Moon, Mercury) — the "Kendradhipati Dosha". [Raman, Ch. III, p. 29]
- **Malefics (Sun, Mars, Saturn, Rahu, Ketu) owning quadrants produce good** — reverse of above.
- **Lords of 3, 6, 8, 11 do evil and cause miseries** unless conjoined with a favourable planet. [Raman, Ch. III, p. 29]
- **Lord of the 8th, if he is also lord of the 1st (which happens for Aries and Libra ascendants), or if conjoined with a benefic, becomes good.** [Raman, Ch. III]
- **Rahu and Ketu produce good results when posited in signs of benefic planets** (Jupiter's Sagittarius / Pisces, Venus's Taurus / Libra, Mercury's Gemini / Virgo). [Raman, Ch. III]

## 1st House — Tanu Bhava (Self / Body / Ascendant)

**Governs:** the physical body, personality, appearance, general life
direction, complexion, temperament, head, brain, longevity indicator
(with the 8th).

**Karakas (natural significators):** Sun (soul-atma), Ascendant lord.

**Strong 1st house = strong life force.** [Raman, Ch. IV, p. 36] —
"If the Ascendant and the Ascendant lord are powerful, good longevity
may be pronounced."

**Common rules:**
- The **sign rising** determines physical constitution — see §Rashi
  descriptions below for per-sign appearance/character notes drawn
  from Raman Ch. V.
- **Malefics aspecting Ascendant weaken the body** unless the aspecting
  planet is also that lagna's Yogakaraka.
- **The 1st house also indicates fame and honour** when its lord is in
  a kendra or trikona and unafflicted.

**Ascendant patterns (Raman, Ch. V, pp. 45-48):**

| Ascendant | Body / character notes |
|-----------|----------------------|
| Aries | Middle stature, weak knees, square face, round eyes, ruddy complexion, full forehead; sociable, quick-tempered, sometimes indolent |
| Taurus | Broad thighs, big face, forgiving disposition, endures hardship, prominent eyes, obstinate, practical, bad temper when aroused |
| Gemini | Curled hair, dark lips, skilled at reading others, elevated nose, likes music and home life, tall slender erect figure |
| (etc — full 12 in Raman Ch. V) | |

## 2nd House — Dhana Bhava (Wealth / Family / Speech)

**Governs:** liquid wealth (bank balance, gold, jewelry, saved money),
family (immediate household), speech, food, right eye, face,
learning capacity in childhood.

**Karakas:** Jupiter (wealth), Mercury (speech), Venus (family
comfort).

**Also a Maraka house** — its lord can inflict death when the death-dasa
arrives. [Raman, Ch. IV, p. 39]

**Common rules from Raman, Ch. VI, p. 41:**
- **Lord of 2 well-disposed = good speaker, capable lecturer.**
- **Mars in 2nd + Mercury in an angle = good mathematician / engineering skill.**
- **Sun or Mars as lord of 2 with Venus or Jupiter = proficiency in logic / mental sciences.**
- **Venus in 2nd = knowledge of astrology / classical arts.**

**Wealth rules (classical, cross-referenced with Raman's 2nd/11th
combinations):**
- Strong 2nd lord in own/exaltation sign in kendra or trikona =
  substantial wealth.
- 2nd lord in 6/8/12 or afflicted by Rahu = wealth loss patterns.
- **Jupiter aspecting 2nd** = protection from real poverty.

## 3rd House — Sahaja Bhava (Siblings / Courage / Communication)

**Governs:** younger siblings, courage, valour, hands and arms, short
journeys, writing and communication, mental strength, initiative,
personal effort.

**Karakas:** Mars (courage, siblings), Mercury (communication when
Mars is weak).

**Classified as Upachaya (growing) house.** Malefics here BUILD
strength over time — Mars in the 3rd is particularly good for
courage-based professions.

**Common rules:**
- **Strong 3rd house + Mars = warrior/entrepreneur/self-made.** [Raman,
  cross-ref Ch. VII on livelihood — dominant 3rd-house planet indicates
  the trade suited to the native.]
- **Lord of 3 in 8th = short life for younger siblings.** [BPHS pattern
  cited by Raman Ch. VIII, p. 51.]
- **3rd house also indicates longevity of elders** (the 3rd is the 8th
  house from the 8th — see "bhavat-bhavam" principle).

## 4th House — Sukha Bhava (Mother / Home / Vehicles / Primary Education)

**Governs:** mother, primary emotional happiness (Sukha = happiness),
home and immovable property (land, house, ancestral property), vehicles,
early education (schooling up to formal degree — higher education is 5th
and 9th), heart region, chest.

**Karakas:** Moon (mother, mind), Mercury (education), Venus (home
comforts, vehicles).

**Ranked #1 by Raman for education:** [Raman, Ch. VI, p. 41] —
"Education is generally ascertained from the 4th house, its lord, and
the Vidyakaraka Jupiter."

**Common rules from Raman, Ch. VI:**
- **Malefics in 4th = broken/interrupted education.**
- **Jupiter in 4th or 10th = high legal education.**
- **Jupiter + Mercury well-situated = strong knowledge of men and matters.**
- **Jupiter + Venus in angles aspected by Mercury = insight into
  philosophical subjects.**
- **The lord of 4 and Jupiter both free from influence of lords of
  3, 6, 11 = sound educational career.**

**Mother:**
- **Malefics in 4th = trouble to mother.** [Raman, Ch. VIII, p. 51]
- **Saturn in 4th with Moon = early death of mother.** [Raman, Ch. VIII]
- **Well-disposed 4th house + strong 4th lord + strong Moon = long-
  lived healthy mother.**

**The 4th is also Digbala for the Moon and Venus** — both planets get
directional strength here. [Raman, Ch. III, p. 27]

## 5th House — Putra / Vidya Bhava (Children / Higher Intellect / Purva Punya)

**Governs:** children, higher intellect (not just IQ — creative and
philosophical mind), mantra and spiritual knowledge, purva-punya
(merit from past lives), speculation and lottery, love affairs,
authorship.

**Karakas:** Jupiter (children, wisdom), Sun (5th-house natural lord
for Leo).

**A Trikona (always benefic) house.** Lords of the 5th are always
good — even natural malefics owning the 5th produce good results.

**Common rules:**
- **Jupiter in 5th = intelligent children, deep wisdom, authorship
  potential.**
- **Malefics in 5th without benefic aspect = delays or difficulty
  with children.**
- **5th and 9th together (both trikonas) = the classical seat of
  fortune (Raja-Yoga potential when trikona lords conjoin).** — see
  §4 in vedic_reference.md for full Raja Yoga rules.
- **The 5th is the house of speculation** — Rahu here without
  benefic support suggests dangerous gambling patterns.

## 6th House — Ripu / Roga Bhava (Enemies / Illness / Debts / Service)

**Governs:** enemies (visible enemies — versus hidden enemies in the
12th), illness and daily health, debts, service (employment under
someone), maternal uncle, litigation, digestion, small animals.

**Karakas:** Mars (enemies), Saturn (service, chronic illness),
Mercury (debts / accounts).

**A Dusthana (evil) house — but also Upachaya (growing).** Malefics
in the 6th are actually good — they defeat enemies and disease. Mars
or Saturn in the 6th is often protective.

**Common rules from Raman, Ch. VIII, p. 52:**
- **Debts (6th) = "if the 6th house is powerful and its lord occupies
  a benefic sign, indebtedness will be minimum."**
- **Lord of 6 in 12 = one causes their own trouble; can also mean
  overseas medical work.**
- **Benefic in 6th weakens the house's protective anti-enemy quality
  — Jupiter or Venus in 6th is not desired for that reason.**

---

## 7th House — Kalatra / Yuvati Bhava (Spouse / Partnerships)

**Governs:** spouse and marriage, business partnerships, foreign
travel and trade, general public interactions, secondary earning
through partnership, kidneys and reproductive organs.

**Karakas:** Venus (spouse for men), Jupiter (husband for women),
Mars (marriage in some traditions).

**Also a Maraka house.** [Raman, Ch. IV] The 7th's lord and any
planet strongly in the 7th can time death events during their dasa.
This does NOT mean the 7th is bad — it means the 7th's dasa is
where lifespan is often measured.

**Also a Kendra (angle)** and Digbala for Saturn. [Raman, Ch. III,
p. 27]

**Common rules:**
- **Strong 7th lord + Venus (for men) unafflicted = harmonious
  marriage.**
- **Malefics in 7th without cancellation = Manglik-like patterns +
  marital delay/friction.** See vedic_reference.md §1 for full
  Manglik rules.
- **Saturn in 7th (Digbala) makes the person patient, marrying
  someone older, or marrying late — but often stable long-term.**

## 8th House — Ayur / Randhra Bhava (Longevity / Transformation / Occult)

**Governs:** longevity (indicator house — see Raman, Ch. IV, p. 37:
"The 3rd and 8th are houses of longevity. Saturn is the indicator of
life."), transformative events (birth, death, sex, initiation),
occult knowledge, inheritances, in-laws (spouse's family), sudden
gains or losses, hidden things.

**Karakas:** Saturn (Ayushkaraka — longevity karaka).

**A Dusthana house** — most malefic of the three. Planets in the
8th are afflicted more than any other placement.

**But it's ALSO the house of transformation and moksha.** Deep
spiritual work often shows an active 8th house.

**Common rules from Raman, Ch. IV:**
- **Strong 8th house + strong 3rd + strong Saturn + strong Lagna =
  long life.**
- **8th lord + Saturn in conjunction = good for long life** (rare
  positive read of the 8th).
- **Moon in 8th house = one of the classical Balarishta combinations
  (early death of child).** [Raman, Ch. IV, p. 36]

## 9th House — Bhagya / Dharma Bhava (Fortune / Father / Higher Learning)

**Governs:** father, fortune (Bhagya = luck/fortune from past-life
merit), religion and dharma, higher learning (university,
postgraduate, PhD), long-distance travel, teachers and gurus,
pilgrimage, philosophy, ethics.

**Karakas:** Sun (father — Raman, Ch. VIII, p. 51 "The 9th house
rules the father"), Jupiter (guru, dharma).

**A Trikona (always benefic) house.** The single most auspicious
house alongside the 1st. Along with the 5th, forms the twin trikona
seat of Raja Yoga.

**Common rules:**
- **Jupiter in 9th = devout, learned, generally fortunate — one of
  the best placements Jupiter can have.**
- **Malefics in 9th afflict the father.** [Raman, Ch. VIII]
- **Sun in 9th with Saturn = early death of father.** [Raman, Ch. VIII,
  p. 51]
- **Strong 9th lord in 1, 4, 7, 10 (kendra) = classical wealth-through-
  fortune pattern.**

## 10th House — Karma Bhava (Profession / Status / Karma)

**Governs:** profession and career, worldly status and rank, actions
in the world (karma), government service, name and recognition,
achievements, father's home (in some readings).

**Karakas:** Sun (authority, status), Mercury (business), Jupiter
(dharmic profession), Saturn (career longevity, service).

**A Kendra (angle) AND Digbala for Sun and Mars.** [Raman, Ch. III,
p. 27] — planets get their peak directional strength here.

**#1 house for judging career.** [Raman, Ch. VII, p. 45] — "Generally
the profession that one pursues is derived from a consideration of
the 10th house (either from the Ascendant or from the Moon, whichever
is powerful) and its lord, planets in the 10th house, the dominant
planet in the horoscope, and the situation of the 10th lord in
Navamsa."

**Career by sign on 10th (Raman, Ch. VII):**
- **Fiery signs** (Aries / Leo / Sagittarius) on 10th → factories,
  fire, army, iron, engineering
- **Earthy signs** (Taurus / Virgo / Capricorn) on 10th → agriculture,
  real estate, mining, stable trade
- **Airy signs** (Gemini / Libra / Aquarius) on 10th → intellectual,
  communication, arts, negotiation
- **Watery signs** (Cancer / Scorpio / Pisces) on 10th → water,
  shipping, chemicals, imagination-based trades

**Common rules:**
- **Sun in 10th (Digbala + own portfolio) = fame, government
  position, natural authority.**
- **Saturn in 10th (Digbala) = long career, often in service /
  government / structured hierarchies; success comes late but is
  durable.**
- **Combust or weak 10th lord = career struggle.**

## 11th House — Labha Bhava (Gains / Elder Siblings / Aspirations)

**Governs:** gains and profits (all forms — income beyond salary,
business gains, gains from investments), elder siblings, aspirations
and wishes, friend circle, gains from mother (11th from father's 9th
= mother; but more literally, income networks).

**Karakas:** Jupiter (gain), Mercury (business gain).

**Classified as Upachaya (growing) house.** Malefics here BUILD gain
capacity over time — Saturn in 11th is often good for accumulated
income.

**But 11th is ALSO a "trik-like" evil house whose lord causes miseries.**
[Raman, Ch. III, p. 29] — "Lords of 3, 6, 8, and 11 do evil and cause
miseries." The 11th's *significations* are good but its *lord* can
create struggle in other areas. Read the two separately.

**Common rules:**
- **Jupiter in 11th = large, easy, ethical gains — one of Jupiter's
  best placements for wealth.**
- **Strong 11th lord = fulfilment of aspirations by mid-life.**
- **Rahu in 11th = large gains but through unconventional means +
  restlessness with what you have.**

---

## 12th House — Vyaya Bhava (Expenditure / Foreign / Moksha)

**Governs:** expenditure and losses, foreign residence and long
journeys abroad, hospitals and confinement, hidden enemies (versus
open enemies in 6th), sleep and bed pleasures, moksha (spiritual
liberation), left eye, feet.

**Karakas:** Saturn (loss, confinement), Ketu (moksha).

**A Dusthana house** — but the ONLY house that gives moksha, so
spiritual practitioners often show an active 12th.

**Common rules:**
- **12th house strong + Ketu here = strong spiritual/moksha inclination.**
- **Rahu in 12th = foreign residence, gains from unusual expenditure
  patterns, or occult / secret involvements.**
- **Sun or Moon in 12th = weakness in father/mother relationship
  respectively, plus tendency toward isolation.**
- **12th's lord in 6, 8, or 12 itself = classical Vipareeta Raja Yoga
  (success from struggle).** See vedic_reference.md §4.4.

## How to phrase in a reading

- **Lead with the karaka + the sign-lord relationship.** Not "your 4th
  house has X planet" (technical — reader glazes over) but "your
  emotional foundation shows…" (concrete life language).
- **Only ONE planet dominates a house's read.** Don't dilute — pick
  the planet with highest impact (lord OR strongest planet in the
  house) and centre the reading on that.
- **Bhavat-bhavam is powerful.** The 3rd from any bhava governs
  that bhava's siblings; the 7th from any bhava governs its
  partnerships; the 9th from any bhava governs its fortune.
- **Malefic in upachaya (3, 6, 10, 11) ≠ bad.** Say so explicitly —
  most seekers will assume malefic = bad and miss the actual reading.
- **NEVER predict death dates from Maraka periods.** Even Raman is
  careful here — the 2nd and 7th houses can bring "death" symbolically
  (endings, transitions) far more often than literal death. Frame as
  "significant closure period" not "life-threatening year".
