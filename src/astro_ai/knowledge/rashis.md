# The 12 Rashis (Zodiac Signs) — Nature, Character, Body

Vedic astrology's twelve rashis (राशि) are the same twelve zodiac signs
as Western — Aries through Pisces — but interpreted through the
**sidereal zodiac** with the **Lahiri ayanamsa** (~24° behind the
tropical zodiac). Which means: someone with Western Sun in Aries is
usually a sidereal Sun in Pisces. This is why Vedic and Western sun-
sign readings can diverge dramatically for the same person.

**Primary sources referenced:**
- BV Raman, *Astrology for Beginners*, Ch. V, pp. 41-45 (henceforth
  **Raman**)
- Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra (**BPHS**) for classical sign
  qualities (Chara/Sthira/Dwiswabhava)
- Muhurta Chintamani for tattva (element) assignments
- Cross-links: houses in `houses.md`; nakshatras in `vedic_reference.md
  §27`; per-sign ascendant rules in `houses.md §1st house`.

**Note on precedence:** in Vedic reading, the **Ascendant (Lagna)
rashi is more important than the Sun-sign rashi**, because the Lagna
determines the entire chart's house structure. The Moon-sign rashi
(Janma Rashi) is second-most-important — it's what daily rashifal
readings are keyed to. Sun-sign is a distant third.

## Six classifications every rashi carries

Each of the twelve rashis inherits six overlapping properties. These
compose into how the sign is read.

### 1. Quality (Chara / Sthira / Dwiswabhava)

| Quality | Signs | Behaviour |
|---------|-------|-----------|
| **Chara** (Movable / Cardinal) | Aries, Cancer, Libra, Capricorn | Executive ability, pioneering, ambition, capacity to make headway against difficulties [Raman, p. 44] |
| **Sthira** (Fixed) | Taurus, Leo, Scorpio, Aquarius | Stability, patience, endurance, diplomacy, fixity of purpose. "They make pages of history." [Raman, p. 44] |
| **Dwiswabhava** (Common / Dual / Mutable) | Gemini, Virgo, Sagittarius, Pisces | Versatility, flexibility, lack of originality. "Superficial knowledge of many things but lacks persistence." [Raman, p. 44] |

### 2. Element (Tattva)

| Element | Signs | Temperament indicator |
|---------|-------|----------------------|
| **Fire (Agni)** | Aries, Leo, Sagittarius | Inspirational, ardent, spirited [Raman, p. 44] |
| **Earth (Prithvi)** | Taurus, Virgo, Capricorn | Practical, grounded, material |
| **Air (Vayu)** | Gemini, Libra, Aquarius | Intellectual, communicative, restless |
| **Water (Jala)** | Cancer, Scorpio, Pisces | Emotional, receptive, imaginative |

**Practical rule from Raman, p. 44:** if the *majority of planets* in a
chart are in signs of one element, that temperament dominates the
whole personality — even if the Lagna is different.

### 3. Gender

| Gender | Signs |
|--------|-------|
| **Male (Purusha)** — odd signs | Aries, Gemini, Leo, Libra, Sagittarius, Aquarius |
| **Female (Stree)** — even signs | Taurus, Cancer, Virgo, Scorpio, Capricorn, Pisces |

Used in specific classical rules (e.g., number of children, gender
prediction) — less useful for general personality reading.

### 4. Direction (Dik)

| Direction | Signs |
|-----------|-------|
| **East** | Aries, Leo, Sagittarius |
| **South** | Taurus, Virgo, Capricorn |
| **West** | Gemini, Libra, Aquarius |
| **North** | Cancer, Scorpio, Pisces |

Used in Vastu and muhurta selection.

### 5. Ruling planet (Rashi Lord)

| Sign | Ruling planet |
|------|---------------|
| Aries (Mesha) | Mars |
| Taurus (Vrishabha) | Venus |
| Gemini (Mithuna) | Mercury |
| Cancer (Karka) | Moon |
| Leo (Simha) | Sun |
| Virgo (Kanya) | Mercury |
| Libra (Tula) | Venus |
| Scorpio (Vrischika) | Mars |
| Sagittarius (Dhanu) | Jupiter |
| Capricorn (Makara) | Saturn |
| Aquarius (Kumbha) | Saturn |
| Pisces (Meena) | Jupiter |

Every planet except Sun and Moon rules two signs. Rahu and Ketu don't
own signs (they're shadow planets); classical texts assign them
"co-lordship" of Aquarius and Scorpio respectively but this is
optional in modern practice.

### 6. Exaltation / Debilitation

| Planet | Exalted in | Debilitated in |
|--------|------------|----------------|
| Sun | Aries | Libra |
| Moon | Taurus | Scorpio |
| Mars | Capricorn | Cancer |
| Mercury | Virgo | Pisces |
| Jupiter | Cancer | Capricorn |
| Venus | Pisces | Virgo |
| Saturn | Libra | Aries |
| Rahu | Taurus (some say Gemini) | Scorpio (some say Sagittarius) |
| Ketu | Scorpio (some say Sagittarius) | Taurus (some say Gemini) |

A planet in its exaltation sign gives peak positive results; in
debilitation, the opposite. **Neechabhanga** (debility cancellation)
rules exist — see `vedic_reference.md §5A` for karakas and BPHS for
the full cancellation logic.

## The 12 rashis — appearance, character, body region

Each rashi below carries the classical description from BV Raman
Ch. V for ascendant-sign reading. **When any of these signs is the
Ascendant** (Lagna) the person tends to embody these physical and
character traits. When the sign is the Moon-sign or Sun-sign the
effect is present but less dominant.

### 1. Aries (Mesha) — मेष

**Body region:** Head, brain.
**Symbol:** The Ram.
**Ruling planet:** Mars.
**Quality/Element:** Movable / Fire.
**Physical (as ascendant):** Middle stature, weak knees, square face,
round eyes, ruddy complexion, full nose and forehead. [Raman, p. 41]
**Character:** Good practicability, sociable, bad temper, sometimes
slow and indolent, does not hesitate to utter falsehood when it
serves them. [Raman, p. 41]
**Practical read:** the classical pioneer — first to move, quick to
fight, quick to make and drop plans. Aries-lagna natives usually look
younger than their age but carry a "reddish/heated" energy that
reads as intensity from across a room.

### 2. Taurus (Vrishabha) — वृषभ

**Body region:** Face, neck, throat.
**Symbol:** The Bull.
**Ruling planet:** Venus.
**Quality/Element:** Fixed / Earth.
**Physical (as ascendant):** Broad thighs, big face, prominent eyes,
generally strong build. [Raman, p. 41]
**Character:** Happy in life, forgiving disposition, endures
hardships, obstinate — "behaves like a bull". Practical ability. Bad
temper only when aroused, but slow to arouse. [Raman, p. 41]
**Practical read:** the classical builder — accumulates over decades,
prizes stability, extremely durable in relationships and career.
Venus rulership gives strong aesthetic sense.

### 3. Gemini (Mithuna) — मिथुन

**Body region:** Arms, shoulders, hands, lungs.
**Symbol:** The Twins (or a couple).
**Ruling planet:** Mercury.
**Quality/Element:** Dual / Air.
**Physical (as ascendant):** Tall, slender, erect figure. Curled
hair, dark lips, elevated nose, arms and fingers long, quick sight,
manual dexterity. [Raman, p. 41]
**Character:** Skilled at reading other people's thoughts. Likes
music and home-keeping. Shy (this surprises Western readers who
associate Gemini with extroversion). [Raman, p. 41]
**Practical read:** the interpreter/communicator, but with an
introspective streak. Excellent at reading rooms; often
underestimates their own communication skill.

### 4. Cancer (Karka) — कर्क

**Body region:** Chest, breast, stomach.
**Symbol:** The Crab.
**Ruling planet:** Moon.
**Quality/Element:** Movable / Water.
**Physical (as ascendant):** Fleshy neck, elevated buttocks, short in
stature, pale complexion, heavy gait. [Raman, p. 41]
**Character:** Attached to home life. Psychic or occult tendencies.
Crooked in views (means indirect, not deceitful). Few sons in
classical framing. Fast walker despite heavy gait. Fond of wealth
accumulation. [Raman, p. 41]
**Practical read:** the emotional home-maker with an occult
undercurrent. Cancer-lagna natives often live near water or return to
their birthplace, and hold on to family/childhood associations more
than any other sign.

### 5. Leo (Simha) — सिंह

**Body region:** Heart, spine, upper back.
**Symbol:** The Lion.
**Ruling planet:** Sun.
**Quality/Element:** Fixed / Fire.
**Physical (as ascendant):** Reddish eyes, large cheeks, broad face,
tall stature. [Raman, p. 42]
**Character:** Arrogant, angry at trifles, firm-minded, generous,
candid, honourable. [Raman, p. 42]
**Practical read:** the classical royal archetype. Leo-lagna
carries themselves with visible dignity, tends toward leadership
roles, but the anger-at-trifles part is real — pride runs hot.

### 6. Virgo (Kanya) — कन्या

**Body region:** Intestines, lower digestive tract.
**Symbol:** The Maiden.
**Ruling planet:** Mercury.
**Quality/Element:** Dual / Earth.
**Physical (as ascendant):** Shoulders and arms drooping, dark hair.
Limited number of issues (children). [Raman, p. 42]
**Character:** Truthful and kindly, good mental abilities,
methodical and critical. [Raman, p. 42]
**Practical read:** the classical analyst and quality-checker. Virgo-
lagna is where perfectionism combines with genuine service capacity.
The "critical" streak in Raman is the eye-for-flaws that makes them
excellent editors, engineers, and doctors.

### 7. Libra (Tula) — तुला

**Body region:** Kidneys, lower back, reproductive system (partial).
**Symbol:** The Scales.
**Ruling planet:** Venus.
**Quality/Element:** Movable / Air.
**Physical (as ascendant):** Lean and frail body, fair appearance,
round face, good complexion, regular features. Slender in youth,
tends toward corpulence with age. [Raman, p. 42]
**Character:** Impartial in argument, brave, sometimes merciless in
judgment. [Raman, p. 42]
**Practical read:** the classical judge/mediator. Venus rulership +
air element gives refined aesthetics and diplomacy, but "merciless"
in Raman is worth noting — Libra isn't just diplomacy for its own
sake; there's a real capacity to render hard verdicts once the
scales have been weighed.

### 8. Scorpio (Vrischika) — वृश्चिक

**Body region:** Genitals, sexual organs, excretory system.
**Symbol:** The Scorpion.
**Ruling planet:** Mars.
**Quality/Element:** Fixed / Water.
**Physical (as ascendant):** Round loins and knees, broad and
expansive eyes, broad chest. Prone to disease in early age. [Raman,
p. 42]
**Character:** Self-reliance, courage, envy, endurance, executive
ability. Will do cruel acts when provoked. [Raman, p. 42]
**Practical read:** the classical warrior-mystic. Mars-rules-water
gives a distinctive "burning-cold" quality — intensely emotional but
armoured. Occult/research/investigative fields are natural fits.

### 9. Sagittarius (Dhanu) — धनु

**Body region:** Hips, thighs.
**Symbol:** The Archer / centaur.
**Ruling planet:** Jupiter.
**Quality/Element:** Dual / Fire.
**Physical (as ascendant):** Long face and neck, big ears and nose,
short stature, great strength. [Raman, p. 42]
**Character:** Liberal, cheerful, enterprising, humane, good-hearted.
Likes physical culture and travelling. [Raman, p. 42]
**Practical read:** the classical philosopher-adventurer. Jupiter
rulership + fire element gives optimism and expansive views. The
"short stature but great strength" is a distinctive Raman
observation.

### 10. Capricorn (Makara) — मकर

**Body region:** Knees, joints, bones.
**Symbol:** The crocodile / makara (not Western goat).
**Ruling planet:** Saturn.
**Quality/Element:** Movable / Earth.
**Physical (as ascendant):** Weak in the lower limbs, prominent nose,
good strength overall despite the weak-knees. [Raman, p. 42]
**Character:** Indolent (can appear so), vindictive if crossed,
"devoid of shame" (means socially unconstrained — will do what needs
doing regardless of appearance), self-possessed, persistent.
Humaneness "absent" in Raman is harsh — modern reading: not
sentimental. [Raman, p. 42]
**Practical read:** the classical long-arc achiever. Saturn rulership
+ earth + cardinal (movable) = the sign of steady persistent building.
The "vindictive" streak Raman notes is real — Capricorns don't forget
slights.

### 11. Aquarius (Kumbha) — कुम्भ

**Body region:** Calves, ankles.
**Symbol:** The Water-Bearer / pitcher.
**Ruling planet:** Saturn.
**Quality/Element:** Fixed / Air.
**Physical (as ascendant):** Tall, sometimes stooping, fair looking,
big buttocks, dark hair. [Raman, p. 42]
**Character:** "Generally commits sinful deeds" (means socially
non-conforming behaviour — the classical framing was harsh here).
Intellectual, inclined to occult matters, forgiving temperament.
[Raman, p. 42]
**Practical read:** the classical outsider/thinker. Saturn +
air-fixed = the sign of unconventional intellectual work — science,
philosophy, occult, anything requiring years of solitary thought.
The "sinful deeds" reading is dated; modern astrologers read this
as social boundary-crossing (not moral wrongdoing).

### 12. Pisces (Meena) — मीन

**Body region:** Feet, lymphatic system.
**Symbol:** Two fishes (often swimming in opposite directions).
**Ruling planet:** Jupiter.
**Quality/Element:** Dual / Water.
**Physical (as ascendant):** Symmetrical and shining body, small and
short limbs. Loses vision if Sun is afflicted. [Raman, p. 43]
**Character:** Fond of spouse, learned, indolent, reserved,
diffident, and dependent. [Raman, p. 43]
**Practical read:** the classical mystic and dreamer. Jupiter +
water-dual = the most spiritually-inclined sign, but the
"diffident and dependent" reading warns of a real pattern —
Pisces-lagna natives often struggle with self-assertion and can
lean heavily on partners or teachers.

## Character modifiers: planets on the Ascendant

Raman notes (p. 43) that the sign-based description above is
significantly modified by any planet in or aspecting the Ascendant.

| Planet on/aspecting Lagna | Adds to appearance/character |
|---------------------------|------------------------------|
| **Sun** | Nobleness to the figure; strong constitution |
| **Moon** | Better proportion and greater delicacy of figure |
| **Mars** | Ruddy complexion, sturdy figure; temperament principally hot and dry |
| **Mercury** | Well-shaped, proportionate stature; hot temperament; yellowish complexion |
| **Jupiter** | Adds dignity, wisdom, and often physical fullness |
| **Venus** | Softness and greater delicacy — "more applicable to female beauty" [Raman, p. 44] |
| **Saturn** | Black and curly hair, tall figure, inactive, narrow chest |

Multiple planets combine — a Cancer ascendant with Venus on the Lagna
reads as: crab-body base (fleshy neck, short) + Venus refinement
(delicacy, symmetry). Both, not one.

## Character modifier: majority-element rule

If most planets in the chart occupy signs of one element, the whole
personality tilts toward that element's temperament. [Raman, p. 44]

- **Fiery majority** → inspirational, ardent, spirited
- **Airy majority** → intellectual, restless, communicative
- **Watery majority** → emotional, sensitive, imaginative
- **Earthy majority** → practical, grounded, sensual, physical

This over-rides the ascendant sign in a big way — a Cancer-lagna
native with 6/9 planets in fire signs reads much more like a fiery
person than a watery one.

## Rashi rulership of body regions (Kalapurusha)

The classical Kalapurusha ("Time-body") assigns each of the 12 rashis
to a body region in order. This is used both for health readings and
for muhurta (choosing times for surgery / medical treatment). [Raman,
Ch. V, p. 46]

| Rashi | Body region |
|-------|-------------|
| 1. Aries | Head |
| 2. Taurus | Face |
| 3. Gemini | Neck / shoulders |
| 4. Cancer | Heart / chest |
| 5. Leo | Stomach |
| 6. Virgo | Waist / intestines |
| 7. Libra | Lower belly / kidneys |
| 8. Scorpio | Sexual organs |
| 9. Sagittarius | Thighs |
| 10. Capricorn | Knees |
| 11. Aquarius | Calves / ankles |
| 12. Pisces | Feet |

**Reading rule:** signs occupied by malefics indicate weakness or
deformity in the corresponding organ; signs with benefics indicate
strength or beauty. [Raman, p. 46]

## How to phrase rashi readings

- **Always lead with the Lagna, not the Sun-sign.** This is the single
  biggest correction to bring Western-trained readers into Vedic
  practice. Sun-sign reading in Vedic is a distant third-most-
  important, behind Lagna and Janma Rashi (Moon-sign).
- **When you cite Raman's physical descriptions, soften the dated
  moral language.** "Sinful deeds" (Aquarius), "cruel acts" (Scorpio),
  "humaneness absent" (Capricorn) — these are 1930s framings. Modern
  usage: "socially non-conforming behaviour", "capable of harsh action
  when provoked", "not sentimental in judgment."
- **The majority-element rule is one of the most useful heuristics.**
  A quick scan of "how many planets in fire signs vs water signs" often
  tells you more about a person than the ascendant reading alone.
- **Note the exaltation/debilitation.** A planet in its exaltation
  sign (Sun in Aries, Jupiter in Cancer, etc.) delivers its karakas
  at their best. Debilitated planets need Neechabhanga cancellation
  to redeem.
