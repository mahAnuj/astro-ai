# Yogas — Classical Planetary Combinations

The most important interpretive layer beyond individual planet
placements. **Yogas are specific combinations of planets that
produce specific results.** Two people can have very similar
individual planet positions but very different lives because one has
a Raja Yoga and the other doesn't.

**Primary sources referenced:**
- P.V.R. Narasimha Rao, *Vedic Astrology: An Integrated Approach*,
  **Ch. 11 "Yogas"** (§§11.1–11.10) (henceforth **Rao**)
- Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra (**BPHS**) — the canonical source
- BV Raman, *Three Hundred Important Combinations* (Raman's yoga
  compendium — separate from *Astrology for Beginners* and not yet
  extracted; noted for future citation)
- Phaladeepika (**Phala**) — cross-cited where relevant
- Cross-links: `grahas.md` for planet properties; `houses.md` for
  house classifications; `aspects.md` for graha-drishti rules;
  `vedic_reference.md §4` for the yogas already covered there
  (Pancha Mahapurusha, Sun yogas, Moon yogas, Vipareeta Raja,
  Nabhasa).

**How to use this file:** for any chart, systematically walk through
the yoga classes below. Note which yogas are present, evaluate their
strength (see §Magnitude below), and let the collection modify the
base house-planet interpretation.

---

## 1. What "yoga" means in Vedic astrology

Sanskrit *yoga* literally means "union" or "combination". In the
astrological sense, it refers to a **specific configuration of two
or more planets** (by conjunction, aspect, exchange, or specific
house placement) that classical texts identify as producing a
specific effect.

**Key distinction:** yoga is not to be confused with the yoga school
of philosophy (union with the divine) or the practice of physical
postures. In astrology, "yoga" is purely a technical term for
planetary combinations.

**When a yoga applies:** Rao is clear (§11.6) that "for a yoga to be
fully present, all the required combinations must be present and the
participating planets must be strong." Weak or afflicted planets
form weakened yogas. This is why two charts can have the "same"
Gajakesari Yoga on paper but very different outcomes.

## 3. Ravi Yogas (Sun combinations) — Rao §11.2

These are extremely common in the rasi chart (because Mercury and
Venus never travel far from Sun) but rarer in divisional charts.
Apply them in D-9 and D-10 for meaningful readings.

### 3.1 Vesi Yoga
- **Rule:** A planet other than Moon in the **2nd house from Sun**.
- **Result:** Balanced outlook, truthful, tall, sluggish. Happy even
  with modest wealth.

### 3.2 Vosi Yoga
- **Rule:** A planet other than Moon in the **12th house from Sun**.
- **Result:** Skillful, charitable, famous, learned, strong.

### 3.3 Ubhayachara Yoga
- **Rule:** Planets other than Moon in **both 2nd and 12th from Sun**.
- **Result:** All comforts. Becomes a king or equal.

### 3.4 Budha-Aditya Yoga (Nipuna Yoga)
- **Rule:** Sun and Mercury together in one sign.
- **Result:** Intelligent, skillful in all works, well-known,
  respected, happy. *Nipuna* means expert.
- **Important caveat:** If Mercury is too close to Sun (within 14°),
  he is combust and the yoga loses power. This yoga is most
  effective in D-10 (career chart) and non-combust Mercury in D-10
  is a strong career-signature.

## 4. Chandra Yogas (Moon combinations) — Rao §11.3

### 4.1 General guidelines from Rao

- Moon in a **quadrant** (1/4/7/10) from Sun → little wealth,
  intelligence, skills.
- Moon in a **panapara** (2/5/8/11) from Sun → average wealth.
- Moon in an **apoklima** (3/6/9/12) from Sun → much wealth,
  intelligence, skills.

- Benefics in **upachaya** houses (3/6/10/11) from Moon: all four →
  great wealth; two → medium; one → little.

### 4.2 Sunapha Yoga
- **Rule:** Planet(s) other than Sun in the **2nd from Moon**.
- **Result:** King or equal. Intelligent, wealthy, famous. Self-earned
  wealth.

### 4.3 Anapha Yoga
- **Rule:** Planet(s) other than Sun in the **12th from Moon**.
- **Result:** King with good looks. Free from disease. Character +
  reputation. Comforts.

### 4.4 Duradhara Yoga
- **Rule:** Planets other than Sun in **both 2nd and 12th from Moon**.
- **Result:** Many pleasures. Charitable. Wealth + vehicles. Good
  servants.

### 4.5 Kemadruma Yoga (the bad yoga)
- **Rule:** No planets other than Sun in 1st, 2nd, or 12th from Moon
  AND no planets other than Moon in quadrants from lagna.
- **Result:** Unlucky, bereft of intelligence and learning, afflicted
  by poverty and trouble. **This bad yoga kills the results of other
  good yogas in the chart, especially Chandra yogas.** Native has to
  work hard and succeed through great efforts.
- **Cancellation:** Aspect of a benefic on Moon, Moon in a quadrant
  from lagna, or Moon in own/exaltation sign can cancel Kemadruma.

### 4.6 Chandra-Mangala Yoga (wealth yoga)
- **Rule:** Moon and Mars together in one sign.
- **Result:** Worldly wise and materially successful. But — Rao notes
  — may earn money through unscrupulous means, and may treat mother
  or other women badly. The wealth signal is real; the moral warning
  is also classical.

### 4.7 Adhi Yoga
- **Rule:** Natural benefics (Jupiter, Venus, Mercury) occupying
  **6th, 7th, and 8th from Moon**.
- **Result:** King, minister, or army chief depending on the strength
  of the planets.
- **Related:** *Lagnaadhi Yoga* is the same rule applied from Lagna
  instead of Moon.

## 5. Pancha Mahapurusha Yogas — cross-reference

Already covered in `vedic_reference.md §4.1`. Quick recap:

Each of the 5 non-luminary tarah grahas (Mars, Mercury, Jupiter,
Venus, Saturn) can produce a Mahapurusha Yoga if it sits **in a
kendra (1/4/7/10) in its own sign or exaltation sign** (from Lagna).
The 5 yogas are named after their planet:

| Planet | Yoga | Kendra + own/exalt rasis |
|--------|------|--------------------------|
| Mars | **Ruchaka** | Ar, Sc, Cp in 1/4/7/10 |
| Mercury | **Bhadra** | Ge, Vi in 1/4/7/10 |
| Jupiter | **Hamsa** | Sg, Pi, Cn in 1/4/7/10 |
| Venus | **Malavya** | Ta, Li, Pi in 1/4/7/10 |
| Saturn | **Sasa** | Cp, Aq, Li in 1/4/7/10 |

These produce "great persons" whose personality is dominated by the
respective planet's tattva (fire for Mars, earth for Mercury, ether
for Jupiter, water for Venus, air for Saturn). Full details in
`vedic_reference.md §4.1`.

## 6. Other Popular Yogas — Rao §11.6

The named yogas most searched by seekers online. These are what an
astrologer might mention specifically by name.

### 6.1 Gaja-Kesari Yoga (elephant-lion yoga — one of the most famous)
- **Rule:** All three conditions:
  1. Jupiter is in a **quadrant from Moon** (1st, 4th, 7th, or 10th)
  2. A benefic conjoins or aspects Jupiter
  3. Jupiter is not debilitated, combust, or in an enemy's house
- **Result:** Famous, wealthy, intelligent. Great character. Liked
  by kings. Rao: "For virtuousness and ever-lasting fame, this is a
  key yoga."
- **Variation:** Some authors count Gaja-Kesari from Lagna instead
  of Moon. If Jupiter is strong, the yoga may work even without a
  benefic's aspect.
- **Search-intent note:** heavily searched by seekers because it's
  the classical "you'll be famous and wealthy" yoga. Real, but
  common enough that having it doesn't automatically make one a
  celebrity — the yoga's *magnitude* matters.

### 6.2 Guru-Mangala Yoga (Jupiter-Mars)
- **Rule:** Jupiter and Mars together, OR in the 7th from each other.
- **Result:** Righteous and energetic. Energies channelled toward
  dharmic paths.

### 6.3 Amala Yoga
- **Rule:** Only natural benefics in the **10th house from Lagna or
  Moon** (no malefics).
- **Result:** Ever-lasting fame. Respected by kings. Luxuries and
  virtue. *Amala* means pure — clean conduct in society (10th =
  career/public role) attracts pure recognition.

### 6.4 Parvata Yoga (mountain yoga)
- **Rule:** Quadrants occupied only by benefics AND 7th & 8th houses
  either vacant or occupied only by benefics.
- **Result:** Fortunate, eloquent, famous, charitable, easy-going,
  humorous.

### 6.5 Kalanidhi Yoga (treasury of arts)
- **Rule:** Jupiter in the 2nd or 5th house, conjoined or aspected
  by both Mercury and Venus.
- **Result:** Character, happiness, good health, wealth, learning.
  Respected by kings. Classical yoga for artistic capacity.

### 6.6 Chamara Yoga
- **Rule:** Lagna lord exalted in a quadrant with Jupiter's aspect,
  OR two benefics join in 7th/9th/10th.
- **Result:** King or respected by kings. Long-lived, scholarly,
  eloquent, learned in many arts.

### 6.7 Sankha Yoga (conch yoga)
- **Rule (form 1):** Lagna lord strong AND 5th and 6th lords in mutual
  quadrants.
- **Rule (form 2):** Lagna lord and 10th lord together in a movable
  sign AND 9th lord strong.
- **Result:** Wealth, spouse, children. Kind, pious, intelligent,
  long-lived.

### 6.8 Bheri Yoga (kettledrum)
- **Rule:** 9th lord strong AND 1st, 2nd, 7th, 12th houses occupied by
  planets.
- **Result:** Wealth, spouse, children. Can be a king. Fame,
  character, virtue.

### 6.9 Kahala Yoga
- **Rule:** 4th lord and Jupiter in mutual quadrants AND Lagna lord
  strong. Alternative: 4th lord exalted/own with 10th lord.
- **Result:** Strong, bold, cunning, leads a large army. Owns
  villages.

### 6.10 Khadga Yoga (sword)
- **Rule:** 2nd lord in 9th house AND 9th lord in 2nd house AND
  Lagna lord in kendra or trikona.
- **Result:** Skillful, wealthy, learned, happy, fortunate,
  intelligent, grateful, mighty.

### 6.11 Kusuma Yoga (flower)
- **Rule:** Lagna in a fixed sign AND Venus in quadrant AND Moon in
  trine with a benefic AND Saturn in 10th house.
- **Result:** King or equal. Charitable, endowed with pleasures.

### 6.12 Subha Yoga (auspicious)
- **Rule:** Lagna has benefics OR *subha kartari* — benefics in 12th
  AND 2nd from Lagna. Kartari = "scissors" (the two houses cast
  their combined influence on the middle house).
- **Result:** Eloquence, good looks, good character.

### 6.13 Asubha Yoga (inauspicious)
- **Rule:** Lagna has malefics OR *paapa kartari* — malefics in 12th
  AND 2nd from Lagna.
- **Result:** Many desires, sinful, enjoys others' wealth.

### 6.14 Matsya Yoga (fish)
- **Rule:** Benefics in Lagna and 9th AND some planets in 5th AND
  malefics in chaturasras (4th and 8th).
- **Result:** Astrologer or seer. Kindness, character, intelligence.
  Learned, famous, austere pursuer.

### 6.15 Kalpadruma Yoga (celestial-tree yoga, aka Parijata Yoga)
- **Rule:** Consider (1) Lagna lord, (2) his dispositor, (3) the
  latter's dispositor in rasi, (4) same in navamsa. If all four
  planets are in quadrants, trines, or exaltation signs, the yoga
  is present.
- **Result:** Becomes a king. Very prosperous, principled, strong,
  kind. *Kalpadruma* is the celestial wish-fulfilling tree.
- **Rare:** All four conditions rarely met. When present, an
  exceptional yoga (Rao's example: Chatrapati Shivaji had this).

## 7. Raja Yogas — Rao §11.7

The classical combinations that give **power and prosperity**. The
most important yoga class in all of Vedic astrology.

### 7.1 The basic rule (Rao §11.7.1)

**Vishnu sits in the quadrants (1/4/7/10). Lakshmi sits in the trines
(1/5/9). When the lord of a quadrant is associated with the lord of a
trine, that association brings the combined blessings of Lakshmi and
Vishnu.** This is a Raja Yoga.

**"Association" here means one of three things:**
1. The two planets are conjoined (in the same sign)
2. The two planets aspect each other via graha drishti (see
   `aspects.md`)
3. The two planets have a **parivartana** (mutual sign exchange —
   e.g. 4th lord in 5th and 5th lord in 4th)

Lagna counts as both a quadrant AND a trine, so its lord is very
important for Raja Yoga formation.

### 7.2 Dharma-Karmadhipati Yoga (the crown-jewel Raja Yoga)

Special case: **the 9th lord (dharma house — the most important
trine) associates with the 10th lord (karma house — the most
important quadrant).**

- **Result:** Sincere, devoted, righteous. Fortunate.
- This is considered the highest-magnitude standard Raja Yoga.
  Presidents, prime ministers, senior CEOs, high-court judges often
  show this combination.

### 7.3 Vipareeta Raja Yoga (success from struggle)

**The 6th, 8th, 12th lords occupying dusthanas or conjoining
dusthana lords.**

- **Result:** Tremendous success, typically after an initial
  struggle. *Vipareeta* means extreme.
- **Logic:** dusthanas (6/8/12) show obstacles. If the dusthana
  lords themselves face dusthana placements, then obstacles run into
  obstacles — the negativity cancels, producing extreme positive
  results.
- **Ideal form:** all three lords (6, 8, 12) together in one
  dusthana or in the 3rd or 11th, with no other planets conjoining
  them. But even one or two dusthana lords in dusthanas can give
  significant Vipareeta Raja Yoga results.
- Full detail: `vedic_reference.md §4.4`.

### 7.4 Magnitude — how strong is a Raja Yoga?

Not all Raja Yogas are equal. Rao §11.7.2 [lines 6151–6234] lists the key
factors, in his order:

1. **Freedom from affliction** — *"The two planets should be free from
   afflictions from functional malefics."* [line 6161]
2. **Closeness of the yoga-forming aspect or conjunction** — *"should be
   close (say, within 6° or so)"* [lines 6163–6164], with a footnote:
   *"Anything greater than 5 or 6 degrees is too large for a Raaja Yoga to
   give its full results."* [line 6256]. Rao's own counter-example: Mercury at
   2° and Venus at 26° of Taurus **do** form Dharma-Karmadhipati Yoga, *"however,
   the two planets are too far apart for this yoga to give its full results"*
   [lines 6165–6171].
3. **Dignity of both planets** — *"should not be combust, debilitated or in an
   inimical house or in bad avasthas (states)"* [lines 6172–6173].

**This is the sourced orb for a yoga, and it is the only one in the corpus.**
6° (or "5 or 6") for a Raja-Yoga conjunction or aspect, from Rao, with a
worked counter-example. Do not confuse it with the 6° `chart.py` uses as a
*combustion* threshold for the Manglik cancellation rule — that one is
UNSOURCED (`grahas.md` §12.4) and the coincidence of the number is a trap, not
a corroboration.

**Rao's Dasa-Varga amsa scale — which Rao attributes to SAGE PARASARA, not
to BPHS as a book** [Rao §11.7.2, plain-`pdftotext` line 6174]:

> *"In addition to the above factors, Sage Parasara recommended looking at the
> amsas occupied by the 2 planets as per Dasa Varga (ten division) scheme."*

**The attribution matters, and this line used to read "(from BPHS)".** Under
the #216 ruling we cite the proximate source we actually read: Rao, who names
Parashara as the authority. We do not cite BPHS, which is not in `data/` and
which `verify_rule_citations.py` fails on unconditionally. Rao's own footnote
(line 6253) does name *"Brihat Paaraasara Horaa Saastram"* while discussing
Saalivaahana — citing *that* is citing Rao's footnote, which is legitimate;
citing BPHS directly is not.

Count in how many of the **ten** Dasa-Varga divisional charts — **D-1, D-2,
D-3, D-7, D-9, D-10, D-12, D-16, D-30, D-60** [Rao §6.6.3, lines 3225–3228] —
**each of the two yoga-forming planets** occupies an own, exaltation, or
moolatrikona sign. The count determines the magnitude of the yoga
[lines 6178–6198]:

| Amsa name | Count | Yoga magnitude |
|-----------|-------|---------------|
| Ordinary | 0-1 | Basic Raja Yoga |
| Parijataamsa | 2 | King who rules his people well |
| Uttamamsa | 3 | Good king with tremendous assets |
| Gopuramsa | 4 | Great king respected by many kings |
| Simhaasanaamsa | 5 | **Great emperor who rules the whole world** |
| Paraavataamsa | 6 | Divine kings only |
| Devalokamsa | 7 | Divine kings only |
| Brahmalokamsa | 8 | Divine only |
| Airaavataamsa | 9 | Divine only (Vishnu incarnations like Rama, Krishna) |

Rao's own ceiling on this ladder, stated immediately after it:

> *"Final Judgment: None of the above factors influences the end result
> completely. We should look at all the factors and make the final judgment."*
> — line 6199

Simhaasanaamsa and above are extremely rare — and Rao's two worked examples are
there precisely to show the count is not decisive in either direction.
**Rajiv Gandhi** [lines 6203–6212] had Raja-Yoga planets in Simhaasanaamsa, the
"emperor of the whole world" rung, but they sat more than 8° apart with
functional malefics on them: *"So the Yoga is pretty weak … In the end, he only
became India's prime minister and not an 'emperor of the whole world'."*
**Akbar** [lines 6213–6225] was a great emperor with his Raja-Yoga planets only
in Uttamaamsa and Paarijaataamsa, because they were *"less than a degree
apart"* and strong in other yogas. Counts 6–9 Rao reserves outright for
*"divine persons such as Svaayambhuva Manu … Brahma (Creator!!) and Vishnu's
incarnations such as Sri Rama and Sri Krishna"* [lines 6195–6198] — so the
ladder's top four rungs are not reachable by a human chart and should never be
rendered to a user as a possible result.

### 7.5 Additional Raja Yoga combinations (Rao §11.7.3)

Rao lists 18 more specific Raja Yoga combinations. Highlights:

- **9th lord and 5th lord in mutual aspect or conjunction** →
  prosperous native.
- **Lagna lord in 5th + 5th lord in Lagna** (parivartana) → Raja
  Yoga.
- **4 or more planets in moolatrikona or exaltation** → becomes a
  king even from a lowly family.
- **Benefics in quadrants + malefics in 3rd, 6th, 11th** → becomes a
  king even from a lowly family.

**Association with rulers** — powerful ministers, bureaucrats,

- **10th lord conjoined or aspected by chara Amatya Karaka (AmK) or
  his dispositor** → important person in a king's court.

## 9. Dhana Yogas (wealth combinations) — Rao §11.9

The 2nd house (dhana), 5th house (purva-punya + speculation), 9th
house (bhagya + fortune), and 11th house (labha + gains) are the
**dhana sthanas** — houses that produce wealth. Combinations between
their lords give Dhana Yogas.

**Common Dhana Yoga rules:**

- **2nd lord in 5th + 5th lord in 2nd** → wealth through speculation
  or investments.
- **11th lord in 2nd + 2nd lord in 11th** → wealth through
  self-earning and business gains.
- **Jupiter aspecting the 2nd house** → protection from real
  poverty; often wealth from Jupiter's domain (teaching, publishing,
  law).
- **9th lord in 11th or 11th lord in 9th** → wealth through
  fortune-based means (inheritance, patronage, chance).
- **All three dhana sthana lords (2/9/11) strong and mutually
  connected** → substantial wealth.

**When to name a Dhana Yoga in a reading:** if a chart shows two or
more of these combinations AND the involved planets are strong AND
Jupiter has some involvement (Jupiter is the karaka of wealth),
Dhana Yoga is present with meaningful magnitude.

## 10. Daridra Yogas (poverty combinations) — Rao §11.10

The mirror image of Dhana Yogas. When the lords of wealth-houses are
weak, afflicted, or placed in dusthanas, Daridra Yogas form.

**Common Daridra Yoga rules:**

- **2nd lord in the 6th, 8th, or 12th house afflicted** → chronic
  scarcity.
- **11th lord in 6th, 8th, or 12th** → income difficulties, gains
  don't materialise.
- **Jupiter debilitated + weak 2nd lord** → wealth-blocking pattern.
- **All dhana sthana lords weak and in dusthanas** → severe daridra
  yoga (extreme scarcity).

**Important honest note:** Daridra Yoga does NOT deterministically
mean the person will be poor. It means wealth requires disproportionate
effort or comes intermittently. Many people with Daridra Yoga still
build lives of adequacy through work; the yoga describes a *pattern*,
not a fate.

## 12. How to phrase yogas in a reading

- **Name the yoga only if it's strong and clearly present.** Rao's
  magnitude rules matter — don't call something a "powerful Gaja-
  Kesari" if the Jupiter is combust or in an enemy's sign.
- **Explain what the yoga produces in plain language.** Don't say
  "you have Malavya Yoga" — say "you have the classical Venus-in-
  kendra combination that gives artistic capacity and material
  comfort".
- **When multiple yogas conflict** (a Raja Yoga + a Kemadruma), read
  the outcome as textured, not contradictory. Both play out at
  different phases of life.
- **Never predict specific outcomes from a yoga alone.** Yogas
  describe potential; the potential lands based on dasha timing,
  dispositor strength, and the seeker's actual choices.
- **Cite yoga names in the UI panel, not in prose.** Users don't
  know what "Ruchaka Yoga" means. In the yogas panel we can display
  the Sanskrit name + effect. In the LLM-generated prose we should
  say "you have a warrior-nature classical combination" and let the
  panel carry the technical detail.

## 13. Yogas in divisional charts — Rao's actual rule (added 2026-10-17, #191)

**All line ranges in this section and in §14 are PLAIN `pdftotext`** (no
`-layout`), giving `data/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf` **20,669** lines — the
fingerprint `SOURCES.md` records. `grahas.md` §12 cites the same book under
`-layout` (16,724 lines) and says so; these ranges will not resolve against
those.

Three statements, and together they are the whole of Rao's doctrine on this:

> **"Many yogas are applicable in divisional charts. When we use yogas in
> divisional charts, we should interpret the results ascribed to a yoga based
> on the matters signified by the divisional chart."**
> — §11.1, lines 5320–5323

> **"When using yogas in divisional charts, adapt the results to the matters
> signified by the chart. All the great results ascribed to some yogas may not
> materialize in reality, due to weaknesses in them. Unless a yoga is very
> strong, all the results cannot expected. Just one yoga cannot make or break a
> personality, unless it is very powerful. In the case of yogas with many
> conditions, partial results may sometimes be experienced if all the
> conditions are not satisfied."**
> — §11.11 Conclusion, lines 6543–6549

> *"Because Mercury and Venus are with Sun or a sign away from him most of the
> time, the following yogas are very common in rasi chart. However, they are
> less common in divisional charts. One can apply these yogas in D-9 and D-10
> in particular."*
> — §11.2 (Ravi Yogas), lines 5350–5353

**So a yoga in a varga is RE-INTERPRETED into that varga's sphere, never
amplified.** Rao's worked instance is Budha-Aditya: *"This yoga is the most
powerful in divisional charts like D-10 … Suppose someone has Sun and Mercury
together in Leo in D-10. Then that person has a powerful Budha-Aditya yoga in
career."* [§11.2.4 "Nipuna Yoga", plain lines **5395–5400**; the same passage
is `-layout` lines 4239–4245, which is how `grahas.md` §12 would cite it] — the yoga's result is *scoped to career* because D-10 is the
career chart, not strengthened because it recurs.

Raja yoga = *"power and prosperity"* (§11.7, lines 6109–6110); dhana yoga = *"abundant riches"* (§11.9, line 6414) |

Where D-9 does appear in Rao's Raja Yogas it is one of a set, and it

Full treatment, including the D-9 vs D-10 significations table and the
divergences from the older uncited material, is in
`divisional_charts.md` → "Yoga strength across the vargas".

Two §1 claims in this file now carry their source:

- The quote in §1 — *"for a yoga to be fully present, all the required
  combinations must be present and the participating planets must be strong"* —
  is verbatim Rao, **§11.6, lines 5798–5800**, and the sentence before it adds
  *"Sometimes the results of a dasa may be felt even if all the required
  combinations are not present."*
- §9's Dhana Yogas: Rao's per-lagna combinations are introduced as *"In
  addition to these general principles, Parasara listed specific combinations
  for various lagnas. In all these combinations, the strength of the
  participating planets decides the magnitude of results experienced."*
  **lines 6419–6421**. So the magnitude rule applies to wealth yogas on the
  same terms as to raja yogas — which is the other half of why
  `prompts/natal.txt:65`'s "raja **or wealth** yoga" phrasing does not get a
  different answer.

Two things Rao's Ch. 11 does **not** give, recorded so nobody cites him for
them:

- **No orb for anything but the Raja-Yoga association.** 6° (§11.7.2) is
  stated for the conjunction/aspect forming a raja yoga. Rao gives no orb for
  Chandra-Mangala, for Gaja-Kesari, or for any other yoga in the chapter.
- **No numeric scoring.** The amsa ladder is the only quantified scale in the
  chapter, and it ranks *outcomes in mythological terms*, not points. Any
  numeric yoga-strength score in this product is our own construct and must
  not be attributed to him.
