# Remedies — The Classical Vedic Approach

The systematic reference for Vedic remedial measures (upāya).
Sourced primarily from P.V.R. Narasimha Rao's *Vedic Astrology: An
Integrated Approach* Ch. 34, which is the most systematic modern
classical treatment. Extends what was already in `vedic_reference.md
§7A` with the full chapter now that we have the extracted PDF text.

**Primary sources referenced:**
- P.V.R. Narasimha Rao, *Vedic Astrology: An Integrated Approach*,
  Ch. 34 "Remedial Measures" (§§34.1–34.6) (henceforth **Rao**)
- Vedic tradition consensus for mantra transliterations (Rao gives
  mantras only in Devanagari to prevent mispronunciation — see
  §Mantra transliteration below for our approach)
- Cross-links: `grahas.md` for planet properties; `vedic_reference.md
  §7A` for the earlier partial extraction that this file now
  supersedes; `houses.md` for functional-benefic determination.

**Scope:** the four categories of remedies Rao identifies (common
sense + pacification via gemstones, good deeds, planetary
propitiation, deity propitiation). Also: safety rules that seekers
absolutely need to know before doing any of this work.

---

## 1. Rao's foundational rules — READ BEFORE ANY REMEDY WORK

Rao is emphatic about these rules and states them upfront. They
should ALWAYS be included in any article or LLM prompt that
recommends remedies.

### 1.1 The purpose of remedies

Remedies do NOT overturn karma. Rao (§34.1): **"Remedial measures
are the measures we take in order to manipulate the exact details
[of a karmic pattern] to be relatively favorable to us."**

Concretely: if a chart shows a vehicular accident is possible in a
period, remedies + common sense may reduce it from a major accident
to a minor one. They do not prevent the karmic event entirely. The
basic pattern set by past-life karma cannot be easily altered; only
the exact expression can.

### 1.2 The devotional framing

Rao opens Chapter 34 with: **"Those who have no belief in Vedic
remedies may skip this chapter."** This is deliberate. Remedies are
DEVOTIONAL acts, not transactional. Anyone treating them as
"paying to make the problem go away" misunderstands the
tradition and often makes the underlying pattern worse (by adding
greed, fear, or manipulation to what should be sincere practice).

### 1.3 The vendor-manipulation warning

If a priest or astrologer demands large sums (tens of thousands of
rupees) for a "nivaran puja" or a "dosha removal" package —
**they are selling anxiety, not tradition**. Genuine classical
remedies are simple and inexpensive. Any article or LLM response
that recommends remedies must explicitly warn against this pattern.

### 1.4 The gemstone-danger rule

**NEVER recommend gemstones without a proper chart consultation.**
Rao (§34.2) is specific: gemstones of *functional malefics* in a
chart should be avoided; gemstones of favorable planets speed up
good results. But determining which is which requires knowing the
Ascendant AND the specific functional benefic/malefic classification
for that Ascendant.

Wearing the wrong stone (especially blue sapphire for Saturn) can
worsen the affliction. This warning must be prominent in any
gemstone-mentioning article.

## 2. The four categories of remedies (Rao §34.1.2)

Rao teaches that planets can be pacified in exactly four ways:

1. **Wearing a gemstone** (only for favorable planets in the chart)
2. **Good deeds** (charity, service, ethical action in the planet's
   domain)
3. **Planetary propitiation** (mantras and stotras)
4. **Propitiation of deities** (worship of the deity ruling the
   planet)

Everything below is organised under these four.

---

## 3. Common-sense remedies (Rao §34.1.1)

Before any ritual remedy: use ordinary awareness.

**When the chart anticipates difficult periods:**
- Drive carefully or avoid unnecessary travel
- Avoid confrontations with authorities
- Delay major decisions if possible
- Reduce financial risk
- Attend to health preventively

**When the chart anticipates good periods:**
- Take considered risks
- Initiate new ventures
- Make major purchases or investments

Common sense is the first line of remedy. Rituals are additional
support, not substitutes for prudent action.

## 4. Gemstones (Rao §34.2)

### 4.1 The safety rule (restated)

Wear only the gemstone of a **functional benefic** in your chart —
not just any planet's stone. Functional benefic determination
depends on your Ascendant. See `houses.md §Classification` for the
Yogakaraka table (which planet is a Yogakaraka for each Ascendant).

**Never wear gemstones of functional malefics.** Never wear
gemstones during maraka dashas of that planet. Both can worsen
outcomes.

### 4.2 Rao's per-planet gemstone table

| Planet | Gemstone | Metal |
|--------|----------|-------|
| Sun | Ruby | Gold |
| Moon | White pearl | Gold |
| Mars | Red coral | Copper |
| Mercury | Emerald | Silver / Platinum |
| Jupiter | Yellow sapphire | Gold |
| Venus | Diamond | Silver / Platinum |
| Saturn | Blue sapphire | Iron (or silver) |
| Rahu | Hessonite (gomedh) | Silver |
| Ketu | Cat's eye | Silver |

### 4.3 Wearing rules

- The ring must be worn for the first time on the **weekday of the
  planet** (Sunday=Sun, Monday=Moon, Tuesday=Mars, Wednesday=Mercury,
  Thursday=Jupiter, Friday=Venus, Saturday=Saturn).
- The planetary strength should be good at the wearing time
  (check current Panchang for Rahu Kaal, planetary hours).
- The planet ruling the gemstone should be strong at wearing time.

### 4.4 Two-gemstone combinations

Two friendly-planet gems can be combined if both planets are
functional benefics AND together form a yoga in the chart. Example
Rao gives: Aries lagna with Sun-Moon conjunction (Raja Yoga) may
wear ruby + white pearl.

### 4.5 Finger conventions

Two schools:

**Traditional:**
- Index finger → Jupiter
- Middle finger → Saturn
- Ring finger → Sun
- Little finger → Mercury / Venus
- Thumb → not used

**Purushartha-based (some modern astrologers):**
- Index → moksha (usually left ringless)
- Middle → dharma
- Ring → artha
- Little → kama
- Wear the ring on the finger of the purushartha you want to
  strengthen (e.g., 7th lord ring on little finger for marriage)

## 5. Good deeds (Rao §34.3)

Good deeds performed in a planet's domain pacify that planet.
Rao's per-planet examples:

| Planet | Good deed to please it |
|--------|------------------------|
| Sun | Visit temple; donate money to a temple; help temple administration |
| Moon | Donate to music institution; help a lady in need |
| Mars | Physical exercise; donate money to a school gym; blood donation |
| Mercury | Donate to organizations of scholars; get blessings from a scholar |
| Jupiter | Respect and donate to a learned Brahmin/priest; donate cows to a priest |
| Venus | Read poetry; help a poet; support artists |
| Saturn | Perform physical labor; help manual laborers; serve the elderly |
| Rahu | Donate to a research organization; go on a pilgrimage |
| Ketu | Meditation; support renunciates |

### 5.1 Grain donations

Each planet rules a specific grain. Donating the grain (particularly
on the planet's weekday) is a classical simple remedy.

| Planet | Grain |
|--------|-------|
| Sun | Wheat |
| Moon | Rice |
| Mars | Toor daal (dark yellow lentil) |
| Mercury | Moong daal (small yellow lentils, dark green skin) |
| Jupiter | Chick peas |
| Venus | Whitish grain (Rao doesn't name specifically) |
| Saturn | Sesame seeds (black) |
| Rahu | Black gram daal (white lentils with black skin) |

Example Rao gives: "Please Jupiter by giving away a big bowl of
chick peas on a Thursday morning."

### 5.2 Fasting

Fasting on the weekday ruled by a planet mitigates the trouble that
planet's placement in a specific house causes:

- **Weekday ruled by upapada lord** (7th house pattern) → mitigates
  marriage troubles
- **Weekday ruled by rajyapada lord** (10th house pattern) →
  mitigates career troubles

By denying the basic necessity of food voluntarily, we reduce other
"punishments" karma might impose.

## 6. Planetary propitiation via mantras (Rao §34.4)

Rao teaches that mantras have specific spiritual efficacy —
combinations of sounds that produce beneficial spiritual waves.
Stotras are hymns of praise; both are used, but with different
requirements.

### 6.1 The pronunciation warning

Rao gives all planetary mantras ONLY in Devanagari script,
specifically to prevent mispronunciation. From §34.4:

> "The stanzas and mantras are being given only in Devanagari
> script used by Sanskrit and Hindi languages. Those who cannot read
> this script should consult a competent astrologer or a Hindu priest
> to learn these stanzas and mantras. This may cause an inconvenience
> to non-Indian readers of this book, but the purpose is to
> discourage mis-pronunciation. Giving an approximate transliteration
> in the Roman script can only encourage mistakes."

**Our approach:** In articles, we cite the widely-accepted
transliteration of the *simple bija mantras* (which are relatively
short and less prone to catastrophic mispronunciation), and
recommend seekers who want the *full nama stotras* consult a
knowledgeable teacher for correct pronunciation. We never invent
transliterations Rao specifically warned against.

### 6.2 Mantra recitation counts (Rao's recommendations)

Each planet's mantra has a specific recommended repetition count
for the full cycle (typically completed over 40+ days):

| Planet | Recommended count |
|--------|-------------------|
| Sun | 6,000 |
| Moon | 10,000 |
| Mars | 7,000 |
| Mercury | 17,000 |
| Jupiter | 16,000 |
| Venus | 20,000 |
| Saturn | 19,000 |
| Rahu | 18,000 |
| Ketu | 7,000 |

The weekday ruled by the planet is ideal for recitation.

### 6.3 Widely-accepted bija (seed) mantras

The bija mantras are short and universally cited across classical
sources. These are safe to transliterate:

| Planet | Bija mantra |
|--------|-------------|
| Sun | *Om Hraam Hreem Hraum Sah Suryaya Namah* |
| Moon | *Om Shraam Shreem Shraum Sah Chandraya Namah* |
| Mars | *Om Kraam Kreem Kraum Sah Bhaumaya Namah* |
| Mercury | *Om Braam Breem Braum Sah Budhaya Namah* |
| Jupiter | *Om Graam Greem Graum Sah Gurave Namah* |
| Venus | *Om Draam Dreem Draum Sah Shukraya Namah* |
| Saturn | *Om Praam Preem Praum Sah Shanaishcharaya Namah* |
| Rahu | *Om Bhraam Bhreem Bhraum Sah Rahave Namah* |
| Ketu | *Om Sraam Sreem Sraum Sah Ketave Namah* |

The full nama mantras (108-name stotras like *Aditya Hridayam* for
Sun) should be learned from a competent teacher for correct
pronunciation and intonation.

## 7. Deity propitiation (Rao §34.5)

Alternative to reciting planet-specific mantras: propitiate the
deity that rules the planet. Rao teaches this is often more
effective because the deity form is more accessible than the
abstract planet.

### 7.1 Rao's planet-to-deity mapping

| Planet | Deities |
|--------|---------|
| Sun | Shiva, Rama |
| Moon | Gauri, Lalita, Saraswati, Krishna |
| Mars | Hanuman, Rudra, Kartikeya (Subrahmanya), Narasimha |
| Mercury | Vishnu, Narayana, Buddha |
| Jupiter | Hayagreeva, Vishnu, Parameswara, Dattatreya, any guru |
| Venus | Lakshmi, Parvati |
| Saturn | Vishnu, Brahma |
| Rahu | Durga, Narasimha |
| Ketu | Ganesha |

### 7.2 Choosing the right deity from planetary combinations

Rao (§34.5): elders intuitively prescribe deities based on the
specific planetary combination:

- **Mercury + Saturn** together → usually a Vaishnava deity
- **Sun** dominant → Shaiva deity (Shiva form)
- **Moon or Venus** dominant → goddess (Devi)
- **Mars** dominant → fiery form (Narasimha, Kartikeya)
- **Rahu** dominant → aggressive deity (Durga)

Example Rao gives: If Rahu is with Mars in Gemini with Saturn +
Mercury aspecting, then Rahu (basic deity Durga) modified by
Saturn+Mercury (Vaishnava) + Mars (angry) → **Ugra Narasimha** (an
angry form of Vishnu). This is what makes deity-selection an art
rather than a table lookup.

## 8. Sri Yantra — the universal safe practice

Not explicitly in Rao's §34 but universally cited in classical
tradition: **Sri Yantra worship** is the classical universal
wealth-and-prosperity generating practice. Safe for anyone to do
without chart consultation:

- Sri Yantra is the geometric form of the goddess Lalita (Sri
  Lakshmi + Sri Tripura Sundari).
- Placement: east or north wall of home; on a raised platform;
  clean cloth.
- Daily practice: light lamp, offer flowers, recite Sri Sukta (from
  Rig Veda) or Lalita Sahasranama.
- No specific planetary contra-indications — Sri Yantra harmonises
  all planetary influences via the goddess principle.

This is what we recommend when seekers want a "safe universal
remedy" without going through chart-specific analysis.

## 9. Divisional chart-based remedy selection (Rao §34.4)

Rao teaches an advanced rule for choosing which planet to propitiate
based on the life area you want to strengthen:

**"Worship the planet owning lagna in the divisional chart
representing the area of life that one wants to improve."**

Examples:
- Want spiritual progress → worship lagna lord in D-20 (Vimshamsha)
- Want pleasures and comforts → worship lagna lord in D-16
  (Shodashamsa)
- Want career strengthening → worship yogakaraka in D-10 (Dashamsa)
- Want marriage improvement → worship 7th lord in D-9 (Navamsha)

This is why our per-endpoint remedy suggestions should key off the
specific area the user is asking about, not just the D-1 chart.

## 10. How to write remedies in articles / LLM responses

Guidance for using this knowledge in articles or ask-endpoint
responses:

1. **Always name Rao's four categories** (common sense, gemstone,
   good deeds, mantra/stotra, deity) so seekers understand the
   options and can choose what fits their disposition.
2. **Lead with good deeds and mantras** — the safe universal
   options. Only mention gemstones with the caveat "requires proper
   chart consultation."
3. **Never recommend a specific gemstone without knowing the
   Ascendant** — if the ask endpoint has access to the user's chart,
   the response can be more specific; if not, keep it generic.
4. **Explicitly warn against expensive "nivaran puja" packages** in
   every remedy article. This is a fixed convention.
5. **Frame remedies as devotional, not transactional.** Words like
   "propitiation" and "pacification" are better than "removing dosha"
   or "cancelling bad karma."
6. **State Rao's core rule explicitly:** "Remedies manipulate the
   exact details of a fated pattern, not the pattern itself."
7. **Recommend Sri Yantra + Sri Sukta as the universal safe fallback**
   when the seeker is anxious but the chart doesn't call for
   anything specific.
