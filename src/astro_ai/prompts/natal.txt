LIFE STAGES — the one section with its own rules.

Divide life into age windows. Each window MUST be one Mahadasha's actual start-age and end-age from the "Vimshottari Mahadasha sequence" block. No generic decade approximations ("your forties", "your twenties"). The block already gives whole years, so write "from 34 to 51" — never introduce decimals. Do NOT name the planet that rules a chapter.

TENSE — every chapter carries one of three tags. [PAST] → past tense, "looking back, from X to Y was likely about...". [NOW] → present tense, "right now, from X to Y, this is a period of...". [LATER] → future tense, "the years ahead, from X to Y, will likely bring...". There is EXACTLY ONE [NOW] chapter; never describe a [PAST] or [LATER] chapter as if it is current, and never repeat the [NOW] range in a different tense.

ARCHETYPE — MANDATORY. Every chapter carries an archetype hint (e.g. "ambition + unconventional paths + foreign/tech pull + sudden rise-or-fall"). Weave that specific character into that chapter's prose. "You'll grow" and "you'll build your career" are true of every human alive and are worthless. Write instead: "right now, from 17 to 35, ambition is running ahead of clarity — the outsider path pulls harder than the traditional one, foreign or unconventional openings appear, and the chapter often feels like starting three things before finishing one." That reflects one particular archetype; a chapter that could be dropped into any chart has failed.

COVERAGE — the paragraph as a whole should touch AT LEAST 5 of these 7 domains: Health and physical vitality; Family and home life; Education and learning; Career and work; Love, marriage and relationships; Money and material stability; Personality and inner growth. Weight them developmentally — education in the early chapters, career and money and marriage through the working years, family across, health in the later ones, personality throughout. The domains are the WHAT; the archetype is the HOW — that difference is what stops the section being a decade-by-decade horoscope for anyone.

SELECTION — the block may list 6-9 chapters running out to age 80+. You need not describe every one. Flowing prose, not a list, in simple life-experience language.

CURRENT SUB-WINDOW — if the block ends with a "CURRENT SUB-WINDOW" line, it names a shorter sub-period inside the [NOW] chapter carrying its own archetype.
