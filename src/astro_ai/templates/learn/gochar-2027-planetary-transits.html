In short

Three things matter in the 2027 sky. Jupiter enters Cancer on 25 January, moves on to Leo on 26 June and reaches Virgo on 26 November. Saturn crosses from Pisces into Aries on 3 June and slips back into Pisces on 20 October. Rahu and Ketu do not change sign at all in 2027: Rahu stays in Capricorn and Ketu in Cancer until 24 May 2028 (true node). How these dates are reckoned

Dates are in Indian Standard Time on the Lahiri (sidereal) reckoning, computed with the Swiss Ephemeris, the same setup this site's charts use. A Panchang can differ by a day when an ingress falls near midnight. Rahu is shown on the true node, as this site's charts use it; almanacs that use the mean node put Rahu's entry into Capricorn on 5 December 2026 and its entry into Sagittarius on 24 June 2028. Consult a Panchang for date-specific events like Sankranti, Guru Peyarchi, or eclipse dates.

What "Gochar" means

Gochar (गोचर) is the Sanskrit term for planetary transit — where planets are moving right now, as opposed to where they sat when you were born. Vedic timing reads the current sky against your natal placements, and the classical method counts every transit as a house from your Janma Rashi (Moon sign), never from the Lagna.

The slow planets — Saturn, Jupiter, Rahu and Ketu — are the ones the classical Gochara table treats as most powerful. This page is the 2027 sibling of our Gochar 2026 guide, which covers the sky from June 2026 into early 2027.

The three biggest 2027 transits

1. Jupiter: Leo, Cancer, Leo, then Virgo

Jupiter — Guru, the Great Benefic — spends 2027 in four stretches, because its motion includes a retrograde loop. On the Lahiri (sidereal) reckoning this site uses, it is in Leo until 25 January, in Cancer from 25 January to 26 June, in Leo again from 26 June to 26 November, and in Virgo from 26 November. Jupiter turns retrograde in Leo on 13 December 2026 and direct again in Cancer on 13 April 2027.

Cancer is Jupiter's exaltation sign, so the five months from 25 January to 26 June are the stretch in which Jupiter itself is strong. The classical Gochara table does not adjust for that: it judges a transit by the house it falls in, counted from your Moon sign, and counts Jupiter as favourable in the 2nd, 5th, 7th, 9th and 11th houses and unfavourable in the other seven. Whether any Jupiter stretch helps you therefore depends on your Moon sign (see the section for each Moon sign below).

Jupiter is a benefic, and a classical rule in this site's reference is that a retrograde planet is read as stronger, not weaker: for Jupiter, deep learning and an expansion of meaning. That applies to the retrograde months up to 13 April.

2. Saturn crosses from Pisces into Aries

Saturn — Shani, the karmic teacher — has been in Pisces (Meena) since 29 March 2025. On 3 June 2027 it enters Aries (Mesha). It turns retrograde there on 9 August, moves back into Pisces on 20 October, turns direct in Pisces on 24 December, and re-enters Aries on 23 February 2028. Aries is Saturn's debilitation sign in the classical dignity table; the Gochara table, again, judges the house from your Moon and not the sign's dignity.

Sade Sati is Saturn in the sign before your Moon sign, in your Moon sign, and in the sign after it, about two and a half years each. Counted that way, the move changes who is in it:

  • Until 3 June (Saturn in Pisces): Aries Moons are in the rising phase, Pisces Moons in the peak phase and Aquarius Moons in the setting phase.
  • 3 June to 20 October (Saturn in Aries): Taurus Moons enter the rising phase, Aries Moons are in the peak phase and Pisces Moons in the setting phase. Aquarius Moons are outside Sade Sati.
  • From 20 October (Saturn back in Pisces): Aries, Pisces and Aquarius Moons are in Sade Sati again, in the phases listed for the first stretch, and Taurus Moons are outside it until Saturn re-enters Aries on 23 February 2028.

Two other Saturn positions are flagged in modern practice: Kantaka Shani (Saturn 4th from your Moon: Sagittarius Moons in Pisces, Capricorn Moons in Aries) and Ashtama Shani (Saturn 8th from your Moon: Leo Moons in Pisces, Virgo Moons in Aries). Those two names are modern usage, not classical terms. What the classical Gochara table does say is that Saturn in the 4th and the 8th from the Moon are both among its unfavourable positions.

3. Rahu and Ketu: no sign change in 2027

The Rahu-Ketu axis moves backward through the zodiac and changes sign about every 18 months. Rahu entered Capricorn (Makara), and Ketu entered Cancer (Karka), on 25 November 2026. That is the true node, the convention this site's charts use. The next change is Rahu into Sagittarius and Ketu into Gemini on 24 May 2028, so for the whole of 2027 the axis sits where it is. The true node wobbles back and forth within Capricorn through the year, but it does not cross into another sign.

Cancer Moons have Ketu in their own sign all year, and Capricorn Moons have Rahu in theirs. For every Moon sign, the house of Rahu is the house of Capricorn counted from the Moon, and Ketu is always opposite it; the table counts both as favourable in the 3rd, 6th and 11th houses and unfavourable in the other nine.

Other notable 2027 transits

  • Mars retrograde (10 January–1 April 2027) — Mars turns retrograde in Leo, backs into Cancer on 10 March, turns direct there on 1 April and returns to Leo on 26 April.
  • Mars in Cancer (10 March–26 April 2027) — Mars is debilitated in Cancer in the classical dignity table. Nothing in the Gochara table changes because of that; it counts Mars by the house it is in, from your Moon.
  • Mercury retrograde — three episodes in 2027 (9 February–3 March, 10 June–5 July, 7 October–28 October). Popular advice for these weeks is to revise rather than sign new commitments; that is practitioner custom, not a rule from the classical texts this site holds.
  • Saturn retrograde (9 August–24 December 2027) — Saturn turns retrograde in Aries and direct again in Pisces. A classical rule in this site's reference reads retrograde Saturn as deep delays with eventual mastery.

What 2027 means for each Moon sign

Below, each Moon sign gets its Saturn phase and then the house in which Saturn, Jupiter, Rahu and Ketu fall, counted from your Moon sign, with the verdict the classical Gochara table gives for that house. A bare house verdict is only a first approximation (the source notes exceptions, for instance through Ashtakavarga, and says Mars and the Sun act in the first 10° of a sign, Jupiter and Venus in the second 10° and Rahu throughout), so weigh it with your dasha and natal chart. Read the one that matches your Janma Rashi (Moon sign), not your Western Sun sign. The themes beside each house are that house's classical significations.

Aries / Mesha

Sade Sati runs through the whole year. Saturn is in the sign before your Moon sign (the rising phase) until 3 June, passes over your Moon sign (the peak phase) from 3 June to 20 October, and is back in the sign before it from 20 October. The classical reasoning is that the Moon signifies the mind and Saturn around it may trouble the mind; the Sade Sati guide covers what each phase asks of you.

  • Saturn: the classical Gochara table counts it as unfavourable in Pisces (12th from you: expenditure, foreign, moksha; until 3 June and from 20 October), unfavourable in Aries (1st from you: self, body; 3 June–20 October).
  • Jupiter: the table counts it as unfavourable in Cancer (4th from you: mother, home, vehicles; 25 January–26 June), favourable in Leo (5th from you: children, higher intellect; until 25 January and from 26 June to 26 November), unfavourable in Virgo (6th from you: enemies, illness, debts, service; from 26 November).
  • Rahu and Ketu: Rahu in Capricorn (10th from you: profession, status) is counted as unfavourable and Ketu in Cancer (4th from you: mother, home, vehicles) as unfavourable, all year.

How to use gochar information wisely

  1. Know your Moon sign, not your Western Sun sign. All Vedic transit reading centres on Janma Rashi.
  2. Match transits to your dasha — a transit the table counts as favourable in a difficult dasha produces mixed results. Both matter. See our Vimshottari Dasha guide.
  3. Don't panic about a house the table counts as unfavourable — Sade Sati is a restructuring, not 7.5 years of misfortune, and every Saturn position ends.
  4. Read the date, not just the sign — the same planet can be in a favourable house for part of the year and an unfavourable one for the rest, as Jupiter's four stretches show.
  5. Check back for 2028 — Saturn re-enters Aries on 23 February 2028 and Rahu moves into Sagittarius on 24 May 2028, so the picture changes early in the new year.

Final thought

Every year the sky has a shape. 2027's shape is a year of crossings: Jupiter changes sign three times and Saturn moves into Aries and back, while Rahu and Ketu hold still. Each crossing is a date on which your house count changes. What Vedic astrology can genuinely offer is a heads-up on which weather to prepare for — the decisions are still yours.
