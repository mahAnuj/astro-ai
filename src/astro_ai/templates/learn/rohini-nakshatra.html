In short

Rohini is the fourth nakshatra — Taurus 10°00' to 23°20'. Its archetype is the favourite of the Moon — beautiful, abundant, growth-oriented. Ruled by the Moon with Brahma the Creator as its deity, symbolised by a chariot or the ever-growing banyan tree, Rohini natives carry the classical qualities of sensual grace and steady accumulation.

Rohini at a glance

  • Sanskrit name: Rohinī (रोहिणी) — from rohit, "the reddish/growing one"
  • Zodiac range: Taurus 10°00' to 23°20' (sidereal, Lahiri ayanamsa)
  • Ruling planet: Moon (Chandra) — Rohini is the Moon's most-favoured nakshatra
  • Deity: Brahma (or Prajapati) — the creator of the universe
  • Symbol: Chariot; or the banyan tree with its aerial roots
  • Gana: Manushya (human temperament)
  • Guna: Rajas (active, driven)
  • Yoni: Serpent (paired with Mrigashira)
  • Nadi: Antya (Kapha / earth-water dosha)
  • Nature (activity): Sthira (fixed) — favourable for building, planting, coronations, gruha pravesh

Why the Moon loves Rohini

Classical Vedic myth places Rohini at the centre of a famous story about the Moon. Chandra, the Moon-god, was married to all 27 nakshatra sisters — but he openly favoured Rohini. His preference was so obvious that the other 26 wives complained to their father Daksha, who cursed Chandra to waste away. The Moon's monthly waxing and waning cycle is the classical Vedic origin story of this curse.

The astrological consequence: Rohini is where the Moon expresses its most beautiful, most abundant, most sensually-alive form. Because the Moon is also exalted in Taurus (see Chandra article — exaltation degree is 3° Taurus), and Rohini spans the second half of Taurus, Rohini natives receive both blessings: the Moon's rulership of the nakshatra plus the Moon's exaltation strength in the sign.

Who is Brahma?

Brahma is the classical creator god of the Vedic trinity (Brahma-Vishnu-Shiva). His role is the continuous act of creation — bringing forth the material world, the forms, the living beings. As Rohini's deity, Brahma gives the nakshatra its distinctive quality of manifesting things into being: children, wealth, artistic works, gardens, buildings, businesses. Rohini natives are makers.

The Rohini archetype: beauty, growth, sensual grace

Rohini natives — born with the Moon between 10°00' and 23°20' of Taurus — tend to:

  • Have visible physical beauty or magnetism — Rohini is classically the "most beautiful nakshatra". Not always conventional beauty, but a warmth others feel
  • Attract abundance — money, food, comfort, friends, admirers. The Moon's favourite of course prospers
  • Grow things patiently — gardens, family, businesses, artistic works. The banyan tree symbol is exact: slow, sustained, deep-rooted
  • Have a strong sensual and aesthetic sense — food, music, decor, fabric, colour, taste. Taurean strength combined with Moon's receptivity
  • Be steady in emotions — where Ashwini flits and Ardra storms, Rohini stays put. The Moon here is at its most stable

The shadow side: attachment. What the Moon loves, the Moon holds. Rohini natives can struggle to let go — of relationships that have ended, possessions no longer needed, family patterns that constrain. The Sthira (fixed) quality of Taurus reinforces this — beautiful when it means constancy, difficult when it becomes stubbornness.

Career: where Rohini thrives

  • Agriculture, horticulture, food production — literal Rohini portfolio; things that grow from the earth
  • Fashion, textiles, cosmetics, jewellery — sensory-aesthetic domains where Rohini's eye pays off
  • Hospitality, restaurants, catering — food + comfort, Rohini's twin gifts
  • Music, dance, performing arts — Moon-plus-Venus (Taurus lord) is the strongest artistic combination in Vedic astrology
  • Real estate and land — Rohini natives often make their wealth through property, especially agricultural or landscaped land
  • Motherhood and caregiving professions — nursing, childcare, midwifery, teaching young children (Brahma-the-creator theme)
  • Banking and finance (particularly the steady-accumulation kind) — Rohini natives are natural savers

Where Rohini natives struggle: high-risk, high-volatility work; rapid pivots; anything requiring detachment from what they've built. Startup founders with Rohini Moon often over-build one product rather than pivoting fast.

Relationships and matching

Rohini is Manushya gana with Antya nadi (Kapha/earth-water).

  • Best matches: Manushya-gana partners with Adi or Madhya nadi — Bharani (Madhya, but same gana; check other kutas), Ardra (Manushya-Adi; different nadi = full Nadi Kuta score), Purva & Uttara Phalguni, Purva & Uttara Ashadha, Purva & Uttara Bhadrapada with different nadi.
  • Challenging matches: Same-Antya-nadi (Krittika, Ashlesha, Magha, Swati, Vishakha, Uttara Ashadha, Shravana, Revati) — lose 8 of 36 Nadi Kuta points. Rakshasa-gana partners create temperament friction.

Rohini natives in relationships bring warmth, comfort, and physical sensuality — the "settle down and build a home together" archetype. Their preferred partners are often those who appreciate steady loyalty over exciting turbulence. The classical challenge: Rohini's attachment can become possessiveness if the partner is more independent-minded.

Health tendencies

Rohini's Nadi is Antya (Kapha — earth-water dosha), and Taurus governs the face, neck, throat in the Kalapurusha (see Kalapurusha reference in rashis knowledge). Classical Kapha-Taurus tendencies for Rohini natives:

  • Weight gain patterns — Kapha metabolism holds onto everything, including calories. Rohini natives often have to actively manage weight as they age
  • Throat and thyroid — Taurus body region; classical Rohini rules over vocal cords, thyroid, upper respiratory
  • Congestion — Kapha excess shows up as mucus, colds, sinus issues
  • Reproductive health emphasis — Rohini is a "fertile" nakshatra, both for children and for creative output. Excess Kapha can also cause fertility difficulty via cysts/blockages
  • Diabetes risk — the classical Kapha metabolic pattern combined with Rohini's love of sweets

Classical support: warming spices in food (ginger, black pepper, turmeric), regular vigorous exercise (Kapha wants to sit still — resist that), reducing sweets and dairy at the first sign of congestion, and cooling summer foods only in moderation.

Vimshottari Dasha implications

Rohini's ruler is the Moon — so Rohini natives start life in a Moon Mahadasha (10 years long). This is a distinctive signature: an early childhood shaped by the mother, by home life, by emotional formation. Followed by the 7-year Mars Mahadasha, which typically kicks in around age 8-10 — a big shift from Moon's gentle emotional education to Mars's active engagement with the world.

Rohini natives often describe childhood as unusually nurturing (or, if the Moon in their chart is afflicted, unusually mother-dominated). Then Mars enters and childhood ends.

Muhurta suitability

Rohini is Sthira (fixed) — one of the most auspicious nakshatras for permanent-nature activities.

  • Gruha pravesh (housewarming) — Rohini is classically ranked as one of the top nakshatras for moving into a new home
  • Coronations and inaugurations — anything you want to "last"
  • Planting trees, laying foundations, starting long-term construction
  • Weddings — Rohini's Sthira quality plus the Moon's fertility associations make it a highly desired wedding muhurta
  • Buying agricultural land or gold — assets meant to appreciate over decades

Avoid: activities requiring quick pivot or detachment — travel starts, short-term speculative trades, breakups.
