In short

The Moon (Chandra) is Vedic astrology's karaka of mind, emotion, mother, memory, and mental peace. Since our psychological wellbeing depends more on Moon than on any other planet, Moon remedies are among the most consequential in Vedic practice. Classical remedies focus on Monday practices, water/milk offerings to Shiva, Chandra Beej mantra, silver items, and pearl gemstone. This guide walks the six most cited Moon remedies with realistic expectations for how they support mental peace and emotional regulation.

Why Moon remedies matter for mental health

The Moon governs manas — the mind that receives, processes, and reflects experience. Read our full Moon in Vedic Astrology guide. When Chandra is weak or afflicted:

  • Emotional turbulence — mood swings, anxiety, feeling ungrounded
  • Sleep disturbance and disturbed dreams
  • Mother-relationship issues — distant, absent, or overbearing mother
  • Water-element imbalances — fluid retention, urinary issues, hormonal irregularities in women
  • Memory issues — forgetfulness, difficulty holding attention
  • Susceptibility to influence from crowds, social media, external opinions

Signs your Moon needs strengthening

  • Moon is debilitated (in Scorpio)
  • Moon is waning (Krishna paksha) and in a difficult house
  • Moon is conjunct or aspected by Saturn (Vish Yoga, depression risk) or Rahu (Grahan Yoga, mental disturbance)
  • Moon is in the 6th, 8th, or 12th houses without benefic support
  • You are in Moon Mahadasha and experiencing prolonged emotional difficulty
  • Life patterns: chronic anxiety, sleep problems, mother-issues, hormonal cycles disrupted, unable to feel emotionally at home anywhere
Important on mental health

Moon remedies support mental peace but are NOT a substitute for professional mental healthcare. If you have symptoms of depression, anxiety disorder, bipolar, PTSD, or any other diagnosed condition, please consult a mental health professional. Remedies work best as adjunct to treatment, not replacement.

1. Milk offering to Shiva on Mondays

Shiva holds the crescent Moon on his forehead — he is the deity most closely associated with Chandra's regulation. Classical practice:

  • Visit a Shiva temple on Monday morning
  • Offer milk (abhishek) to the Shivalinga
  • Recite "ॐ नमः शिवाय" (Om Namah Shivaya) 108x
  • Sit quietly for 10 minutes after the abhishek

If temple visit is impractical, home practice: pour milk over a Shivalinga (or Shiva image) on Monday morning, do the mantra count, sit quietly. Effect: builds a Monday emotional-reset ritual that many practitioners describe as the calmest hour of their week.

2. Somvar (Monday) vrat

The Monday fast is Chandra's peak devotional day. Options:

  • Somvar vrat — regular Monday fast (partial: one meal only, no rice, no lentils)
  • Solah Somvar vrat — 16 consecutive Mondays, particularly for unmarried women seeking a good spouse or for those with severe emotional difficulty
  • Shravana Somvar — Mondays during Shravana month (July-August) are peak

3. Chandra Beej Mantra

ॐ श्रां श्रीं श्रौं सः चन्द्राय नमः
Om Shraam Shreem Shraum Sah Chandraya Namah

108x daily on Mondays. Sit facing north-west. Best time: evening after sunset when Moon becomes visible.

Some traditions end the mantra with "Chandramase Namah" instead of "Chandraya Namah" — both are grammatically valid dative forms (Chandramas vs Chandra); use whichever your family lineage prescribes.

4. Silver items and pearl-related practices

  • Wear silver — ring, chain, or anklet. Silver is Moon's metal.
  • Drink water from a silver glass in the morning
  • Sleep with a small silver item under the pillow if experiencing sleep issues
  • Pearl (Moti) gemstone — Moon's classical gemstone; safer than most but still verify Ascendant compatibility. Compatible: Cancer (Moon's own sign), Taurus (Moon's exaltation), Pisces (friendly). Avoid Scorpio Ascendant — Moon is DEBILITATED in Scorpio and Pearl can amplify weakness rather than strengthen. General rule: never wear a gemstone of a functionally-malefic planet for your Lagna without a proper chart consultation.

5. Monday charity — specific items

  • Rice — Moon's classical primary offering
  • Milk and milk products
  • White clothes
  • White flowers
  • Silver items (if means allow)

Weekly consistency > one-off large donation.

Moon rewards steadiness. Not intensity, not sacrifice — just showing up on Monday, every Monday.

6. Serving mother and elderly women

Classical practice: if mother is living, serve her consciously — touch her feet, seek her blessings, listen to her stories. If mother is deceased, perform her shraddha annually. Also: serve elderly women (grandmothers, neighbours, temple sevikas) — Moon rewards those who honor the maternal archetype.

7. Mind-quieting practices — the modern extension

Chandra's essential nature is unstable mind. Classical Ayurvedic + Yogic practices for stabilising manas complement the ritual remedies:

  • Nadi Shodhana (alternate-nostril breathing) — 10 minutes twice daily
  • Brahmi (herb) — traditional Ayurvedic mind-tonic
  • Regular sleep-wake schedule aligned with sunrise/sunset — Moon governs biological rhythm
  • Limiting social media before bed — modern equivalent of "protecting the mind from unstable inputs"

Timeline for Moon remedy effects

  • Weeks 1-2: Sleep quality often the first shift
  • Weeks 3-8: Emotional reactivity decreases; small things feel less catastrophic
  • Months 3-6: Structural mental patterns shift; mother-relationship healing
  • Year 1+: Baseline emotional wellbeing meaningfully higher; less swept away by external turbulence

Reading Moon remedies honestly

Moon remedies work because most operate on rhythm (Monday), body (silver water), and attention (mantra). All three support the nervous system's own regulation systems. Combine with professional mental healthcare when needed. Check your Moon's placement in your free Janma Kundli on Shubh Bhagya.
