In short

Uttara Phalguni is the twelfth nakshatra — Leo 26°40' to Virgo 10°00'. Its archetype is the patron of contracts and lasting friendship — commitment, generosity to those in one's care, the reliable elder. Ruled by the Sun with Aryaman (patron of pacts, friendship, and hospitality) as its deity, symbolised by the back legs of a bed, Uttara Phalguni natives carry the classical qualities of steadfast reliability and generous patronage.

Uttara Phalguni at a glance

  • Sanskrit name: Uttarā Phalgunī (उत्तरा फाल्गुनी) — "the latter reddish one"; the second of the paired Phalguni nakshatras
  • Zodiac range: Leo 26°40' to Virgo 10°00' (spans two signs)
  • Ruling planet: Sun (Surya)
  • Deity: Aryaman — the Aditya (solar deity) of patronage, contracts, hospitality, and lasting friendship
  • Symbol: Back legs of a bed (the pair-completion of Purva Phalguni's front legs)
  • Gana: Manushya (human temperament)
  • Guna: Rajas (active, driven)
  • Yoni: Cow (paired with Uttara Bhadrapada)
  • Nadi: Adi (Vata / air dosha)
  • Nature (activity): Sthira (fixed) — favourable for building, planting, coronations, gruha pravesh

Aryaman — patron of the friendship contract

Aryaman is one of the Adityas (twelve solar deities), and specifically the Aditya of lasting bonds — the sacred contract of marriage, the pledge of friendship, the vow of patronage, the covenant of hospitality between host and guest. Where Purva Phalguni's Bhaga governs the enjoyment of what is present, Aryaman governs the keeping of what has been promised.

Classical Vedic society placed enormous weight on Aryaman's contracts. The oath given between friends, the pledge between husband and wife, the promise made to a guest — these were held to be as sacred as ritual offerings to the gods themselves. Aryaman's blessing rests on those who keep their promises across time; his displeasure falls on those who break them.

The bed completed: Purva + Uttara

Purva Phalguni's symbol is the front legs of a bed; Uttara Phalguni's is the back legs. Together they complete the bed as a single Vedic image of romantic love: pleasure at the head, commitment at the foot. Purva is where lovers meet; Uttara is where they stay. Classical texts consistently rank the Purva–Uttara Phalguni pair as one of the strongest marriage-matching combinations in the entire nakshatra system, because the two halves genuinely complete each other.

The Uttara Phalguni archetype: reliable, generous, patron-like

Uttara Phalguni natives — born with Moon between 26°40' Leo and 10°00' Virgo — tend to:

  • Be extraordinarily reliable — if they say they'll do something, it gets done. This is the single most-cited Uttara Phalguni quality in classical texts
  • Take on patronage of people and causes — the "she puts three friends through college", "he funds his brother's business", "she mentors the next generation" patterns
  • Be excellent hosts — the Aryaman quality of hospitality; guests feel taken care of
  • Value long-term relationships over short-term intensities — friendships of 30+ years, marriages that outlast the culture around them
  • Have strong ethical instincts about promises — breaking a commitment causes them real distress
  • Combine Leo's warmth with Virgo's precision — the two-sign span gives Uttara Phalguni a distinctive "generous but organised" quality

The shadow side: over-obligation. Because Uttara Phalguni natives take their commitments so seriously, they can accumulate more than they can carry. The Aryaman quality can tip into codependence — carrying friends and family who should be carrying themselves. Classical remedy: the mature Uttara Phalguni learns to distinguish between commitments they've chosen and obligations others have assumed.

Career: where Uttara Phalguni thrives

  • Legal work, especially contract law — Aryaman's classical portfolio
  • HR, executive coaching, mentorship roles — patronage in institutional form
  • Diplomatic and ambassadorial work — the "hospitality between nations" archetype
  • Long-term project management, construction management — Sthira quality applied
  • Marriage and family therapy, wedding officiation — Aryaman's marital-pact domain
  • Nonprofit leadership, foundation management — sustained patronage of causes
  • Medicine — especially long-term care (geriatrics, chronic conditions, family practice)
  • Family business steward roles — the sibling everyone trusts to run the enterprise fairly

Where Uttara Phalguni natives struggle: fly-by-night startup work; roles requiring easy detachment from commitments; environments where reliability is a naive weakness rather than a professional strength.

Relationships and matching

Uttara Phalguni is Manushya gana (human temperament) with Adi nadi (Vata/air).

  • Best matches: Manushya-gana partners with Madhya or Antya nadi — Rohini (Antya), Bharani (Madhya), Purva Phalguni (same gana Madhya nadi — the classical pair-completion match), Purva/Uttara Ashadha, Purva/Uttara Bhadrapada. Purva Phalguni + Uttara Phalguni is consistently ranked one of the strongest Ashtakoot pairings in all classical literature.
  • Challenging matches: Same-Adi-nadi (Ashwini, Ardra, Punarvasu, Hasta [half], Jyeshtha, Mula, Shatabhisha, Purva Bhadrapada) lose 8 Nadi Kuta points. Rakshasa-gana partners create Gana-Kuta friction.

Uttara Phalguni natives in partnership bring the Aryaman qualities directly: steady loyalty, real reliability, generous care for the partner's wellbeing. They tend to marry once and stay. Their preferred partners are those who value being taken care of AND who take care in return — one-sided giving eventually depletes even Uttara Phalguni's reserves.

Health tendencies

Uttara Phalguni's Nadi is Adi (Vata / air). Standard Vata concerns:

  • Nervous-system emphasis — anxiety and insomnia patterns; the "carrying responsibility for others" pattern accumulates in the nervous system
  • Joint stiffness — classical Vata dryness in joints; especially the shoulders (the "carrying the weight" region)
  • Digestive irregularity — Vata makes digestion unpredictable
  • Sun's rulership adds heart considerations — cardiovascular health matters especially for Uttara Phalguni's Leo half
  • Overall constitution generally strong — Sun's brightness combined with Virgo's discipline gives a body that responds well to care

Classical support: regular routine (Vata hates irregularity), warming and moist foods, sesame or coconut oil self-massage, meditation to process the burden of others' problems (rather than carry them somatically), regular cardiovascular exercise.

Vimshottari Dasha implications

Uttara Phalguni's ruler is the Sun — so Uttara Phalguni natives begin life in a Sun Mahadasha (6 years). This is the same signature as Krittika, and the same interpretation applies: early life strongly shaped by the father figure, by authority themes, or by an early sense of role/mission. The child who's already "the responsible one" at age 5 is often an Uttara Phalguni.

Followed by the 10-year Moon Mahadasha — usually kicking in during early childhood. This is often experienced as the "emotional education" phase — Moon brings care for the mother, the home, the family bond. Uttara Phalguni natives often describe their childhood as unusually shaped by their parents in sequence (father in the Sun period, mother in the Moon period).

Muhurta suitability

Uttara Phalguni is Sthira (fixed) — one of the most auspicious muhurtas for permanent commitments.

  • Weddings and engagement ceremonies — one of the top classical wedding muhurtas (Aryaman's own domain)
  • Signing long-term contracts — employment, business partnerships, real-estate purchases
  • Gruha pravesh (housewarming) — the "settling in for the long term" ceremony
  • Coronations, inaugurations, taking up institutional roles
  • Planting trees, laying foundations, starting long-term construction
  • Founding a nonprofit or long-term charitable initiative

Avoid: activities requiring quick pivots, short-term speculative trades, temporary arrangements meant to be dissolved.
