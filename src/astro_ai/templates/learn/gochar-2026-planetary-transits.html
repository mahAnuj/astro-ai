In short

2026 is a rare year in the sky. Jupiter enters its exaltation sign, Cancer, on 2 June 2026, which happens only about once every 12 years — though it does not stay in one stretch: Jupiter moves on to Leo on 31 October 2026 and returns to Cancer on 25 January 2027. Saturn continues its testing stay in Pisces (Sade Sati active for three rashis). And on 25 November 2026, the Rahu-Ketu axis makes a major shift into Capricorn-Cancer, resetting the karmic direction for millions. Here's the full picture, plus what it means for each Moon sign.

What "Gochar" means

Gochar (गोचर) is the Sanskrit term for planetary transit — where planets are moving right now, as opposed to where they sat when you were born. Your natal chart is fixed; the sky isn't. Vedic timing works by reading how the current sky (gochar) interacts with your natal placements.

The most consequential transit reading comes from watching slow-moving planets against your Janma Rashi (Moon sign). Fast-movers (Moon, Mercury) change signs daily or weekly and shape daily mood. Slow-movers — Saturn, Jupiter, Rahu, Ketu — shape entire life chapters.

The three biggest 2026 transits

1. Jupiter exalted in Cancer (June–October 2026, and again January–June 2027)

Jupiter — Guru, the Great Benefic — enters its exaltation sign, Cancer (Karka), on 2 June 2026. Jupiter takes about 12 years to circle the zodiac, so it reaches its exaltation sign only about once every ~12 years. Exaltation makes Jupiter itself strong, but the classical Gochara table judges a transit by the house it falls in, counted from your Moon sign, so whether this transit helps you depends on your Moon sign (see the outlook for each Moon sign below).

The stay is split in two, because Jupiter's motion includes a retrograde loop. On the Lahiri (sidereal) reckoning this site uses, Jupiter is in Cancer from 2 June to 31 October 2026, in Leo from 31 October 2026 to 25 January 2027, back in Cancer from 25 January to 26 June 2027, and then in Leo again. If you are reading this after 31 October 2026, Jupiter has already moved on to Leo and returns to Cancer in late January 2027. A Panchang can differ by a day when an ingress falls near midnight.

What Jupiter in Cancer can amplify, for those whose Moon sign puts it in a favourable house (the 2nd, 5th, 7th, 9th or 11th from the Moon: Gemini, Pisces, Capricorn, Scorpio and Virgo Moons):

  • Wealth accumulation for anyone with well-placed 2nd or 11th houses
  • Children — especially first-child conception for couples in Jupiter's timing windows
  • Marriage — Jupiter is the marriage karaka for women; strong exalted Jupiter often coincides with weddings, engagements, or marital deepening
  • Higher learning and dharma — advanced degrees, teaching roles, spiritual initiations
  • Public respect — recognition, awards, leadership positions

Jupiter's aspect from Cancer reaches the 5th house (Scorpio), the 7th (Capricorn), and the 9th (Pisces) from Cancer. So, while it is in Cancer, it directly blesses those signs' natives.

2. Saturn in Pisces (March 2025 – around June 2027)

Saturn — Shani, the karmic teacher — is transiting Pisces (Meena), where it arrived on 29 March 2025. On the Lahiri (sidereal) reckoning it moves into Aries on 3 June 2027, slips back into Pisces on its retrograde path on 20 October 2027, and re-enters Aries on 23 February 2028. This creates Sade Sati or its variants for three Moon signs:

  • Aquarius Moons — Sade Sati setting phase (Uttara). The final 2.5 years of a 7.5-year Sade Sati that began in early 2020. Integration phase. The worst is behind you; consolidate.
  • Pisces Moons — Sade Sati peak phase (Madhya). Saturn is exactly on your Janma Rashi. Emotionally the most intense of the three phases. Big life restructuring, health emphasis on mind and body, family responsibility often increases.
  • Aries Moons — Sade Sati rising phase (Arambha). Saturn entered the sign before your Janma Rashi in March 2025. You're in the early part of a 7.5-year chapter. Notice what's being subtracted from your life; don't rebuild yet.

Plus, Sagittarius Moons are still in Kantaka Shani (Saturn 4th from Moon — pressure on home, mother, comfort). And Leo Moons are in Ashtama Shani (Saturn 8th from Moon — testing shared resources, hidden anxieties, sometimes health).

3. Rahu-Ketu axis shift into Capricorn-Cancer (late November 2026)

The Rahu-Ketu axis moves in ~18-month cycles. Currently (mid-2026) Rahu is in Aquarius (Kumbha) and Ketu in Leo (Simha). On 25 November 2026, the axis shifts — Rahu enters Capricorn (Makara) and Ketu enters Cancer (Karka). That is the true node, the convention this site's charts use; almanacs that use the mean node give 5 December 2026. This shift will define the karmic direction for the following 18 months.

Who feels this shift most:

  • Capricorn Moons — Rahu enters your rashi. Ambition amplifies; unconventional paths open. Watch for obsessive tendencies.
  • Cancer Moons — Ketu enters your rashi. Detachment period begins. Emotional withdrawal or spiritual awakening likely.
  • Aquarius Moons — Rahu departs, Sade Sati setting phase active. The intense Rahu-in-Rashi period ends; consolidation deepens.
  • Leo Moons — Ketu departs (relief for many), Ashtama Shani still active. Complex year.

Other notable 2026 transits

  • Mars in Taurus (21 June–2 August 2026) — a passage of about six weeks, with no retrograde loop (Mars does not turn retrograde in 2026). Slow-burn energy. Real estate matters activate.
  • Mars in Cancer (18 September–12 November 2026) — Mars is debilitated in Cancer. Aggression softens; but also decisions become harder, action stalls. Not the time to launch high-conflict initiatives.
  • Jupiter retrograde (13 December 2026 – 13 April 2027) — Jupiter turns retrograde in Leo and re-enters Cancer on 25 January 2027 while still retrograde. Jupiter's blessings turn inward. Great period for spiritual practice, revising life philosophy, and internal wealth building rather than external show. Some marriage plans may pause during this window.
  • Mercury retrograde — three episodes in 2026 (Feb–Mar, Jun–Jul, Oct–Nov). Standard advice: don't sign new contracts, do revise old ones, back up your data.

What 2026 means for each Moon sign

Below is a compact year-outlook per rashi based on the major transits above. Where a rashi's reading mentions Jupiter in Cancer, it applies while Jupiter is in Cancer (2 June–31 October 2026 and again 25 January–26 June 2027); in between, Jupiter is in Leo. The classical Gochara table judges each planet by the house it occupies, counted from your Moon sign. It does not adjust for Jupiter's strength in Cancer, and a bare house verdict is only a first approximation (the source notes exceptions, for instance through Ashtakavarga), so weigh it with your dasha and natal chart. Read the one that matches your Janma Rashi (Moon sign), not your Western Sun sign.

Aries / Mesha

Sade Sati rising phase (Arambha) is under way. Saturn entered the sign before yours in March 2025. Notice what feels unstable in your life — job, relationships, health — and let subtraction happen without panic. Jupiter in Cancer (4th from you) is counted as unfavourable by the classical Gochara table, so read it as a stretch that asks for care around home, mother and property, not as a promise of gain there. The Rahu-Ketu shift on 25 November puts Rahu in your career house (10th, Capricorn), which the table also counts as unfavourable for Rahu: treat sudden career offers with scrutiny rather than assuming they are windfalls.

Taurus / Vrishabha

Mars in your rashi from 21 June to 2 August — about six weeks of high physical energy, real estate deals possible, but watch for accidents. Jupiter in Cancer (3rd from you) is counted as unfavourable by the classical Gochara table, so pace courage and communication rather than pushing them. Rahu shifts to your 9th house (Capricorn) on 25 November, which the table also counts as unfavourable for Rahu: give travel plans, teachers and beliefs extra scrutiny rather than assuming a helpful one arrives.

Gemini / Mithuna

Jupiter in Cancer (2nd from you) — wealth expansion, family harmony, sweet speech, potential income growth. This is one of the best transits for you in the year. Mercury (your Lagna lord) retrograde three times — pace yourself in communications. Rahu enters your 8th (Capricorn) on 25 November — hidden matters surface; occult interests deepen.

Cancer / Karka

Two huge stories. Jupiter enters exaltation in your rashi on 2 June 2026, moves on to Leo on 31 October, and returns to Cancer from 25 January to 26 June 2027, so it comes in two stretches rather than one. Exaltation makes Jupiter strong, but the classical Gochara table judges the house from your Moon, and Jupiter in Cancer (1st from you, your own rashi) is counted as unfavourable there: read it as Jupiter's themes of family, children, wisdom and dharma bearing on you personally, to be handled with care rather than as a guaranteed windfall. AND Ketu enters your rashi on 25 November, beginning a 1.5-year period of detachment and inner clarity. The two together — Jupiter's emphasis + detachment — often produce meaningful life pivots. Not one to waste.

Leo / Simha

Ashtama Shani active (Saturn 8th from your Moon in Pisces) — expect inner testing, hidden anxieties, possible health flare-ups. Ketu in your rashi (until 25 November) adds detachment — many Leos feel spiritually restless this year. Jupiter in Cancer (12th from you) is counted as unfavourable by the classical Gochara table; it suggests foreign travel, spiritual retreats, or expenses on wisdom-building. Ketu's departure on 25 November brings relief.

Virgo / Kanya

Jupiter in Cancer (11th from you) — one of the best Jupiter placements possible for you. Gains, wealth, network expansion, aspiration fulfilment. Rahu in Aquarius (6th from you) — professional battles winnable, service-oriented work rewarded. Mercury (your Lagna lord) retrograde impacts you more than average.

Libra / Tula

Jupiter in Cancer (10th from you) is counted as unfavourable by the classical Gochara table, so do not assume promotion or recognition: treat career moves and public standing with care and weigh them against your dasha. Saturn's Pisces transit is 6th from you (dusthana) — enemies and health matters need attention but you can overcome. The Rahu shift to Capricorn (4th from you) on 25 November may bring home/mother events.

Scorpio / Vrishchika

Jupiter in Cancer (9th from you) — one of the strongest transits for fortune, teachers, dharma, and father-related matters. Long-planned pilgrimages happen this year. Higher learning favoured. Saturn in Pisces (5th from you) tests children and creative pursuits; be patient. Rahu shifts to your 3rd (Capricorn) on 25 November — courage tested, communication amplifies.

Sagittarius / Dhanu

Kantaka Shani active (Saturn 4th from your Moon in Pisces) — home, mother, and inner peace under pressure. Jupiter (your Lagna lord) in Cancer (8th from you) is counted as unfavourable by the classical Gochara table and is complicated — deep transformation, occult interests, sometimes inheritance dealings. The 25 November Rahu-Ketu shift affects your 2nd (wealth) and 8th (transformation) houses.

Capricorn / Makara

Rahu enters your rashi on 25 November — begins an 18-month period of amplified ambition, obsessive pursuits, and unconventional paths. Life direction may shift dramatically late in the year. Jupiter in Cancer (7th from you) is excellent for marriage and business partnerships while it is in Cancer. Saturn (your Lagna lord) in Pisces (3rd from you) helps courage and communication.

Aquarius / Kumbha

Sade Sati setting phase (Uttara) — final 2.5 years. The most intense part is behind you. This is the integration year — consolidate what the transit has taught you. Rahu departs your rashi on 25 November, ending an intense 18-month period. Jupiter in Cancer (6th from you) is counted as unfavourable by the classical Gochara table, so do not count on it to settle enemies or disputes; handle conflicts with care. Health improves as year progresses.

Pisces / Meena

Sade Sati peak phase (Madhya) — Saturn exactly on your Moon. The most emotionally intense year. Family responsibility increases; health of mind and body needs care; big life restructuring possible. Balancing this — Jupiter in Cancer (5th from you) blesses children, creativity, and love. The 25 November Rahu-Ketu shift moves the axis off your immediate area — some relief.

Gochar doesn't decide your year. It tells you which weather to prepare for.

How these dates are reckoned

Dates are in Indian Standard Time on the Lahiri (sidereal) reckoning, computed with the Swiss Ephemeris. A Panchang can differ by a day when an ingress falls near midnight. Rahu is shown on the true node, as this site's charts use it; almanacs that use the mean node put Rahu's entry into Capricorn on 5 December 2026. Consult a Panchang for date-specific events like Sankranti, Guru Peyarchi, or eclipse dates.

How to use gochar information wisely

  1. Know your Moon sign, not your Western Sun sign. All Vedic transit reading centres on Janma Rashi.
  2. Match transits to your dasha — a favourable transit in an unfavourable dasha produces mixed results. Both matter. See our Vimshottari Dasha guide.
  3. Don't panic about difficult transits — Sade Sati doesn't ruin lives; it restructures them. Kantaka Shani and Ashtama Shani test specific themes but always end.
  4. Time big decisions with the sky — marriage plans in Jupiter's favourable transit windows, career moves when Sun/Mars support your 10th house, investments during Mercury direct periods.
  5. Update your reading periodically — this article is written for 2026; the transits shift substantially in 2027 when Saturn moves to Aries (around June) and the Rahu-Ketu axis moves further. Return for a fresh 2027 outlook.

Final thought

Every year the sky has a shape. 2026's shape is unusually favourable in one direction (Jupiter's exaltation in Cancer) and unusually testing in another (Saturn in Pisces / Sade Sati). Both are real. Neither is destiny. What Vedic astrology can genuinely offer is a heads-up on which weather to prepare for — the decisions are still yours.
