In short

Pushya is the eighth nakshatra — Cancer 3°20' to 16°40'. Classical Vedic tradition calls it the most auspicious nakshatra of the zodiac. Ruled by Saturn with Brihaspati (guru of the gods) as its deity, symbolised by a cow's udder or lotus, Pushya natives carry the classical qualities of nourishment, discipline, and spiritual wisdom.

Pushya at a glance

  • Sanskrit name: Puṣya (पुष्य) — from push, "to nourish"
  • Zodiac range: Cancer 3°20' to 16°40' (sidereal)
  • Ruling planet: Saturn (Shani)
  • Deity: Brihaspati — guru of the gods, teacher of dharma
  • Symbol: A cow's udder (source of nourishing milk); or the lotus flower
  • Gana: Deva (godly temperament)
  • Guna: Tamas (deep, contained quality — despite Sattva-flavoured deity)
  • Yoni: Sheep (paired with Krittika)
  • Nadi: Madhya (Pitta / fire dosha)
  • Nature (activity): Kshipra (swift/light) — favourable for learning, transactions, trade, medicine

Why Pushya is called "the most auspicious"

Classical Vedic astrologers rank Pushya at or near the top of every "best nakshatra for X" list. The combination is unique: Saturn's rulership (discipline, endurance) + Brihaspati's deity (dharma, wisdom) + Deva gana (refined temperament) + Cancer sign (nurture, home, emotional depth) — this collection of factors is more universally favourable than any other single nakshatra can claim.

Because of this, ancient muhurta selection rules often use "when the Moon is in Pushya" as the default good time for almost any auspicious activity — with only a small list of exceptions.

A specific exception: never marry during Pushya

Classical muhurta texts contain one striking exception to Pushya's blanket auspiciousness: weddings should not happen when the Moon is in Pushya. The stated reason varies by text — some say because Pushya is associated with Guru (Brihaspati) whose grace prefers scholarly rather than romantic beginnings; others say because Cancer's emotional intensity plus Saturn's slowness makes for a heavy start to a marriage. Whatever the reason, muhurta calendars will explicitly exclude Pushya from wedding date suggestions. Every other kind of auspicious start is welcomed on Pushya days.

The Pushya archetype: nourisher, teacher, quietly deep

Pushya natives — born with Moon between 3°20' and 16°40' of Cancer — tend to:

  • Feed people — literally (they cook, they insist you eat, they remember your food preferences) and figuratively (they nurture ideas, projects, and careers of people around them)
  • Take responsibility for others' welfare — Saturn rulership gives a strong "someone has to make sure it gets done" instinct, applied to community/family care
  • Be genuinely wise despite few claims — Brihaspati's grace shows up as understanding rather than performance
  • Have a quiet, contained personality — Tamas guna means the depth is hidden; you have to sit with a Pushya native to see it
  • Move through life with unusual steadiness — Saturn's discipline + Cancer's emotional depth = fewer dramatic swings than most people
  • Be respected in their community — the "we all look to X for advice" role

The shadow side: over-responsibility. Because Pushya natives are so reliably competent and so quietly caring, others often over-delegate to them. Pushya can end up carrying multiple people's lives and feeling depleted. The classical remedy is boundary-setting — Saturn's own portfolio, so it's actually a natural fit for a mature Pushya native.

Career: where Pushya thrives

  • Teaching, especially long-form (school teachers, professors, gurus) — Brihaspati's classical portfolio
  • Medicine and healthcare — the nourishing/healing archetype applied
  • Food and hospitality — literal Pushya work (chefs, nutritionists, dieticians, restaurant owners)
  • Financial advising, wealth management, banking — Saturn's classical portfolio applied to protecting others' resources
  • Priesthood, religious teaching, ritual work — Brihaspati is the classical guru of ritual
  • Agriculture and dairy work — cow-udder symbolism taken literally; Pushya was traditionally the herder's nakshatra
  • Real estate and property management — Cancer + Saturn = home + structure
  • Long-term project management — the Saturn discipline applied to years-long undertakings

Where Pushya natives struggle: fast-moving glamour-industry work; roles where being underestimated is a career killer; environments that don't value slow careful competence.

Relationships and matching

Pushya is Deva gana (refined) with Madhya nadi (Pitta/fire).

  • Best matches: Deva-gana partners with Adi or Antya nadi — Ashwini (Adi), Punarvasu (Adi — often cited as very compatible), Hasta (Antya), Shravana (Antya), Revati (Antya). Punarvasu-Pushya is a classical strong pairing: same gana, different nadi, complementary Jupiter–Saturn temperaments.
  • Challenging matches: Same-Madhya-nadi (Bharani, Mrigashira, Purva Phalguni, Chitra, Anuradha, Purva Ashadha, Dhanishta, Uttara Bhadrapada) lose 8 Nadi Kuta points. Rakshasa-gana partners create Gana Kuta friction.

Pushya natives in love are deeply loyal and slow to leave — Saturn's endurance combined with Cancer's emotional attachment. The classical happy Pushya partnership is one where the partner appreciates the quiet care and doesn't require constant demonstration. Pushya natives can find "high-drama" partners exhausting.

Health tendencies

Pushya's Nadi is Madhya (Pitta). Combined with Cancer's watery emotional signature and Saturn's slowness:

  • Chest and lung emphasis — Cancer rules the chest region; Pushya specifically rules the chest cavity
  • Digestive fire imbalances — Pitta digestive intensity; classical Pushya health rules mention acidity, ulcers, and gall bladder issues
  • Weight-holding pattern — Cancer + Saturn tends to accumulate; watch for weight gain from mid-30s onward
  • Emotional-eating pattern — Cancer's emotional signature routed through the food-relationship makes Pushya natives prone to comfort-eating during stress
  • Overall constitution: strong endurance, slower recovery from acute illness

Classical support: cooling foods for Pitta (coconut, cucumber, mint, ghee), emotional processing that doesn't route through food (meditation, journalling), moderate but regular exercise, avoiding excess spice/salt/sour tastes.

Vimshottari Dasha implications

Pushya's ruler is Saturn — so Pushya natives begin life in a Saturn Mahadasha (19 years long — the longest of all Mahadashas). This is a distinctive signature: an early life shaped by responsibility, family duty, sometimes early economic pressure, and an unusually serious/mature childhood. The child who "acts like a small adult" is often a Pushya.

Followed by the 17-year Mercury Mahadasha — usually kicking in during late teens or early twenties. This is often experienced as the "life lightens up" phase — Mercury brings intellectual expansion, communication skills, and often a career pivot into teaching/writing/analytical work.

Muhurta suitability

Pushya is Kshipra (swift/light) AND the classical "most auspicious" nakshatra. Suitable for essentially any auspicious activity EXCEPT weddings:

  • Starting a new business, signing contracts, opening a shop
  • Beginning medical treatment, buying medicine, starting therapy
  • Starting a course of study, admissions, first day at a new school
  • Buying property, gruha pravesh (Pushya is one of the top gruha pravesh muhurtas)
  • Buying gold or investing in long-term assets — "Pushya Nakshatra shopping" (especially for gold) is a well-known Indian folk tradition
  • Spiritual initiations, mantra initiation, guru-disciple beginnings
  • Religious ceremonies, yajnas, temple visits

Explicitly avoid: weddings. Every other major auspicious event is welcomed.
