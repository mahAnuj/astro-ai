In short

The 11th lord — the planet ruling the sign in your 11th house (Labha Bhava) — directs your gains, income, ambitions, and network. Where it sits across the 12 houses shapes HOW gains arrive. 11th lord in own house or 2nd brings direct income accumulation; in 10th brings career-driven gains; in 5th brings creative/speculative gains; in 12th brings foreign/spiritual gains that don't accumulate materially. This guide walks all 12 placements with classical interpretations.

What the 11th house governs

  • Gains — from all sources (salary, investments, network, business)
  • Long-term goals and ambitions
  • Elder siblings
  • Friends and social network
  • Fulfilment of desires (Kama purushartha's outcome)

1st house — gains through personal reputation and ambition

Ambition becomes core identity. Gains flow toward the personal brand. Self-driven, entrepreneurial. Natural leadership tendencies.

2nd house — gains accumulate as family wealth

Gains directly join accumulated wealth pool. Family businesses. Speech-driven earnings (public speaking, sales, teaching, media).

3rd house — gains from effort, siblings, communication

Media, writing, publishing, sales, siblings' businesses. Short-travel and initiative-driven gains.

4th house — gains from property, mother, home business

Real estate, home-based businesses, vehicles. Mother's role in career or family business.

5th house — gains from speculation, children, creativity

Stock market, entertainment, creative pursuits, teaching/coaching. Children may indirectly bring gains. Advisory work.

6th house — gains through service, competition, litigation

Service industries, law, medicine, employment (rather than business). Recurring income patterns. Enemies-become-clients paradox.

7th house — gains through spouse and partnerships

Marriage brings prosperity. Partnership businesses flourish. Foreign spouse or foreign partnerships may bring international income.

8th house (dusthana) — gains from hidden sources

Inheritance, insurance, joint ventures, occult/research income. Sudden windfalls interspersed with sudden losses. Requires financial discipline; not smooth wealth.

9th house — gains from higher education, fortune, dharma

Publishing, teaching, dharmic institutions, foreign connections, father's blessings. Ethical wealth-building. Often successful in law, higher education, religious/spiritual services.

10th house — gains directly through career and authority

Very strong placement. Career and gains are unified. Reputation drives income. Government or corporate high-position wealth.

11th house — 11th lord in own house

Strongest placement. Multiple simultaneous income streams. Networks are prosperous. Group ventures succeed. Elder siblings supportive.

12th house (dusthana) — gains flow outward

Foreign gains, expenditure on travel/luxury/charity, spiritual retreat income. Wealth doesn't accumulate materially; spent well or badly depending on chart.

11th lord placement isn't just where money comes from — it's what the person's karma wants to accomplish with that money.

Softeners and modifiers

  • Dignity — own sign / exalted vs. debilitated / combust
  • Jupiter aspect — the primary wealth blessing
  • 2nd + 11th lord conjunction or exchange — classic Dhana Yoga; amplifies both
  • Saturn aspect — delays but disciplines the gain pattern
  • Rahu conjunction — sudden, foreign, unusual gain patterns
