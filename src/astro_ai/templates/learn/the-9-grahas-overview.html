In short

Vedic astrology reads your life through nine planets — the seven classical grahas (Sun, Moon, Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, Saturn) plus the two lunar nodes Rahu and Ketu. Each governs a specific set of themes, rules a weekday and direction, has a sign where it flourishes and one where it struggles, and comes with a classical remedy toolkit. This guide is your starting map.

Why nine and not eight?

Astronomy today counts eight planets. Vedic astrology counts nine grahas — the seven that the ancient sages could see with the naked eye (Sun and Moon are counted as planets in astrology, though they are a star and a satellite in astronomy), plus Rahu and Ketu, the two mathematical points where the Moon's orbit crosses the Sun's path.

Rahu and Ketu aren't physical bodies you can point to in the sky. But eclipse-prediction — one of the earliest concrete achievements of Vedic astronomy — depends on tracking them precisely, and classical texts observed that when the Moon crosses these points, human behaviour and events cluster in specific ways. So Vedic astrology treats them as full grahas with karmic weight.

Uranus, Neptune, and Pluto — used in modern Western astrology — aren't part of the classical Vedic system because they weren't visible without telescopes. Contemporary practitioners occasionally reference them, but classical prediction relies on the nine.

The classification: benefic, malefic, neutral

Every planet carries a temperament. Classical texts sort them roughly:

  • Natural benefics — planets whose energy tends to bring comfort: Jupiter (highest benefic), Venus, Mercury (when not conjunct malefics), and the waxing Moon.
  • Natural malefics — planets whose energy tests, restricts, or accelerates: Saturn, Mars, the Sun, Rahu, Ketu, and the waning Moon.
"Malefic" is not "bad"

Modern Vedic teachers translate the classical papa graha as "malefic" for lack of a better word, but the meaning is closer to "difficult, testing, growth-inducing". Saturn is a malefic; Saturn also builds the most durable careers. Mars is a malefic; Mars is also the karaka of courage. Malefic energy is what makes people grow. It hurts on the way; it works over time.

Beyond the natural classification, each planet also becomes a functional benefic or malefic based on which houses it rules for your specific rising sign. A functional analysis is chart-specific and often reverses the natural label — that's for a deeper reading, not this overview.

The nine grahas, at a glance

Here is each graha with the essentials — what it governs, its weekday, its direction, the sign where it's exalted (behaves at its best), where it's debilitated (behaves at its worst), and the classical gemstone associated with it.

1. Sun (Surya, Ravi)

Governs: soul, self, vitality, father, authority, government, career visibility, physical health (heart, eyes).
Weekday: Sunday · Direction: East
Exalted in: Aries (Mesha) · Debilitated in: Libra (Tula)
Own sign: Leo (Simha) · Gemstone: Ruby (Manikya)
Karaka for houses: 1st (self), 5th (fame, children), 9th (father, dharma), 10th (career)

2. Moon (Chandra, Soma)

Governs: mind, emotions, mother, home, peace, nurturing, monthly cycles, water.
Weekday: Monday · Direction: North-west
Exalted in: Taurus (Vrishabha) · Debilitated in: Scorpio (Vrishchika)
Own sign: Cancer (Karka) · Gemstone: Pearl (Moti)
Karaka for houses: 4th (mother, home), plus mind across the whole chart

3. Mars (Mangal, Kuja, Bhauma)

Governs: courage, energy, siblings (younger), property, competition, surgery, muscle strength.
Weekday: Tuesday · Direction: South
Exalted in: Capricorn (Makara) · Debilitated in: Cancer (Karka)
Own signs: Aries (Mesha), Scorpio (Vrishchika) · Gemstone: Red Coral (Moonga)
Karaka for houses: 3rd (courage, younger siblings), 6th (enemies)

4. Mercury (Budha)

Governs: intellect, communication, learning, trade, mathematics, wit, short travel.
Weekday: Wednesday · Direction: North
Exalted in: Virgo (Kanya) · Debilitated in: Pisces (Meena)
Own signs: Gemini (Mithuna), Virgo (Kanya) · Gemstone: Emerald (Panna)
Karaka for houses: 10th (career, especially in communication or trade)

5. Jupiter (Guru, Brihaspati)

Governs: wisdom, dharma, wealth, children, teachers, religion, higher learning, fortune.
Weekday: Thursday · Direction: North-east
Exalted in: Cancer (Karka) · Debilitated in: Capricorn (Makara)
Own signs: Sagittarius (Dhanu), Pisces (Meena) · Gemstone: Yellow Sapphire (Pukhraj)
Karaka for houses: 2nd (family wealth), 5th (children), 9th (fortune, guru), 11th (elder siblings, gains)

6. Venus (Shukra)

Governs: love, marriage, comfort, luxury, art, music, beauty, vehicles, feminine principle.
Weekday: Friday · Direction: South-east
Exalted in: Pisces (Meena) · Debilitated in: Virgo (Kanya)
Own signs: Taurus (Vrishabha), Libra (Tula) · Gemstone: Diamond (Heera)
Karaka for houses: 7th (spouse, marital bliss)

7. Saturn (Shani)

Governs: discipline, time, structure, karma, longevity, labour, restriction, the elderly, chronic conditions.
Weekday: Saturday · Direction: West
Exalted in: Libra (Tula) · Debilitated in: Aries (Mesha)
Own signs: Capricorn (Makara), Aquarius (Kumbha) · Gemstone: Blue Sapphire (Neelam)
Karaka for houses: 8th (longevity, endurance), 12th (loss, isolation)
Full deep-dive: Shani (Saturn): The Misunderstood Karmic Teacher

8. Rahu (North Node)

Governs: outward hunger, ambition, obsession, foreign lands and cultures, unconventional paths, technology, illusions.
Weekday: — (associated with Saturday in modern practice) · Direction: South-west
Exalted in: Taurus (some say Gemini) · Debilitated in: Scorpio (some say Sagittarius) · Exaltation for Rahu/Ketu is disputed across classical texts.
Gemstone: Hessonite / Gomedh
Note: Always retrograde in Vedic convention. Never rules a sign of its own.

9. Ketu (South Node)

Governs: inward release, spirituality, moksha (liberation), past-life karma, occult knowledge, detachment, sudden endings.
Weekday: — (associated with Tuesday in modern practice) · Direction: North-west
Exalted in: Scorpio (disputed) · Debilitated in: Taurus (disputed)
Gemstone: Cat's Eye (Lehsunia)
Note: Always retrograde. Sits exactly 180° opposite Rahu, always. Doesn't rule a sign.

A quick note on Rahu and Ketu

Because they're mathematical points rather than physical planets, Rahu and Ketu don't behave like the classical seven — they don't rule signs, and their exaltation / debilitation is genuinely contested across texts. What every school agrees on: they matter enormously. Rahu Mahadasha (18 years) and Ketu Mahadasha (7 years) are two of the most transformative chapters in a life. The classical friendships and enmities

Grahas have relationships. Some planets are natural friends, some are enemies, some are neutral — and this affects how they behave when they occupy each other's signs or aspect each other. The classical grid (Parashara's Naisargika Maitri):

  • Sun: friends Moon, Mars, Jupiter · enemies Venus, Saturn · neutral Mercury
  • Moon: friends Sun, Mercury · enemies (none) · neutral Mars, Jupiter, Venus, Saturn
  • Mars: friends Sun, Moon, Jupiter · enemies Mercury · neutral Venus, Saturn
  • Mercury: friends Sun, Venus · enemies Moon · neutral Mars, Jupiter, Saturn
  • Jupiter: friends Sun, Moon, Mars · enemies Mercury, Venus · neutral Saturn
  • Venus: friends Mercury, Saturn · enemies Sun, Moon · neutral Mars, Jupiter
  • Saturn: friends Mercury, Venus · enemies Sun, Moon, Mars · neutral Jupiter

Rahu and Ketu aren't included in Parashara's natural-friendship grid because it predates their formal inclusion in the graha family. Modern practice uses ad-hoc rules for them.

How grahas move (and why speed matters)

The nine grahas move at very different speeds through the zodiac. This dramatically affects how often you feel their influence:

  • Moon — fastest. About 13° per day. Changes sign every ~2.5 days. This is why Panchang and daily Rashifal shift day-to-day.
  • Sun — ~1° per day. Changes sign every ~30 days (Sankranti days mark these transitions).
  • Mercury, Venus, Mars — variable, days to weeks per sign.
  • Jupiter — ~1 year per sign. When Jupiter changes sign (Guru Peyarchi), whole life-domains shift for several years.
  • Saturn — ~2.5 years per sign. Slow enough that Sade Sati (7.5 years) is a major life chapter.
  • Rahu & Ketu — ~1.5 years per sign, always moving retrograde in Vedic convention.

The slowest planets — Saturn, Jupiter, Rahu, Ketu — shape life chapters. The fastest — Moon — shapes daily mood. That's why Rashifal is Moon-based and dashas are structured around Vimshottari (which starts from Moon's exact position at birth).

The faster the graha, the more it colours today. The slower the graha, the more it colours the decade.

What to do with all of this

Reading your own chart is a lifetime skill, not a weekend course. But this overview gives you the vocabulary. When you generate your Janma Kundli and see "Jupiter in Cancer, house 4", you can now translate:

  • Jupiter — wisdom, wealth, children
  • In Cancer — exalted (best possible sign)
  • House 4 — home, mother, inner peace

Reading: exalted Jupiter blessing the home and inner life. That's a substantial gift — usually a nurturing home, a wise or influential mother, and inner contentment across life. That's how a single line of chart data becomes a real insight.

Final thought

The nine grahas aren't nine judges deciding your fate. They're nine colours in a palette you were painted with. Some colours are warmer, some colder; some make the picture bright, some make it deep. Understanding which colours are strong in your painting is what a good reading does — not to explain what happened, but to help you use what you already have.
