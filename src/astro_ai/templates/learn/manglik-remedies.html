In short

Manglik Dosh (Mangal Dosh) remedies focus on softening Mars's placement in the six houses that qualify (1, 2, 4, 7, 8, 12) that classical tradition flags for marriage friction. The most powerful remedies aren't expensive rituals — they're Hanuman worship (Mars's presiding deity), Tuesday practices, Kumbh Vivah ritual for severe cases, and — most consequential — Ashtakoot Guna Milan matching to find compatible partners. This guide walks the seven classically-cited Manglik remedies, with the honest note that cancellation rules (already applied in your Kundli) often mean no remedy is even needed.

Check first: is your Manglik genuinely uncancelled?

Before choosing any remedy, use our free Manglik Dosh checker to see whether your chart's Manglik is:

  • None — Mars is not in any qualifying house. No remedy needed.
  • Cancelled — Mars is in a qualifying house BUT classical cancellation rules apply (Cancer/Leo Lagna Yogakaraka rule, Jupiter aspect, own-sign Mars, matching with another Manglik of comparable severity). Remedies optional, mostly for peace of mind.
  • Mitigated — Some softening factors present but not full cancellation. Remedies advised.
  • Present — Full Manglik with no cancellation. Remedies + careful matching recommended.

1. Hanuman worship — Mars's presiding deity

Classical tradition assigns Hanuman as Mars's presiding deity. Hanuman was born under a Mars-influenced lagna in Ramayana lore, and his very nature (physical strength + selfless service) embodies Mars at his best.

  • Hanuman Chalisa — recite 1x daily, 7x on Tuesdays, 11x during severe periods
  • Hanuman Ashtak or Bajrang Baan — additional advanced hymns
  • Tuesday temple visit — offer sindoor + oil + betel leaves to Hanuman
  • Recite "ॐ हं हनुमते नमः" (Om Ham Hanumate Namah) — 108x on Tuesdays

Effect: Hanuman practice reliably tempers Mars's aggressive edge. Anger reduces, patience increases, arguments in marriage settle faster.

2. Mars's other deities — Narasimha, Kartikeya, Rudra

The classical table gives Mars not one deity but four: Hanuman, Rudra (a fierce form of Shiva), Kartikeya (Subrahmanya) and Narasimha. When Mars dominates a chart, tradition reaches for a fiery form — Narasimha or Kartikeya — rather than a gentle one. Narasimha is an angry form of Vishnu, so if Vishnu is your family's deity, this is the route that stays within it.

  • Narasimha — the man-lion form of Vishnu, a fierce protector
  • Kartikeya (Subrahmanya) — the war god, the other fiery form tradition names for a Mars-dominant chart
  • Rudra — the fierce form of Shiva

The table is a starting point, not a prescription: the deity that fits depends on the whole chart, and on which one you feel drawn to. Vishnu devotions your family already keeps (Ekadashi fasts, Satya Narayan Katha) are worth continuing as devotion in their own right. They are a devotional tradition, not one of the deities the classical table gives Mars, so we do not count them among the remedies.

3. Mars Beej Mantra

ॐ क्रां क्रीं क्रौं सः भौमाय नमः
Om Kraam Kreem Kraum Sah Bhaumaya Namah

108x daily on Tuesdays. Sit facing south. This is the shastric Mars mantra; complement it with Hanuman practices for maximum effect.

4. Tuesday charity — specific items

  • Toor daal (arhar / split pigeon pea) — Mars's classical grain
  • Jaggery (gud)
  • Red clothes and red flowers
  • Copper items

Weekly consistency on Tuesdays. Frame mentally as "for my Mars."

5. Kumbh Vivah — for severe Manglik before marriage

A folk-remedial practice for severe uncancelled Manglik BEFORE marriage (we have found no classical source for it): Kumbh Vivah (marriage to a kumbh — clay pot) or Peepal Vivah (marriage to a peepal tree). The ritual "consumes" the first Manglik-marriage karma, allowing the second (actual) marriage to be softer.

Practical notes:

  • Performed by qualified priests, typically before the marriage tilak/roka ceremony
  • Cost from legitimate priests: ₹2,000-10,000
  • Not universally accepted — some modern astrologers question its efficacy; others swear by it. Discuss with your family astrologer.
  • Often more valuable as psychological closure than as ritual mechanic — many report a sense of "starting fresh" after it
The strongest Manglik remedy is finding a partner whose chart naturally balances yours — not a ritual, but a good match.

6. Marriage matching — the truest Manglik remedy

Classical tradition emphasises that Manglik matching Manglik cancels the dosha — when the two are of comparable severity. The dosha "meets itself" and neutralises, and it is the evenness of the match that does the neutralising; a mild Manglik paired with a severe one is not the same thing, and tradition does not promise it cancels. Tradition also says "comparable" and leaves the threshold open, so we set one and name it as ours: Shubh Bhagya grades Manglik low, moderate or high, counts equal or adjacent grades as comparable, and will not cancel a low against a high. 7. Behavioural remedies — Mars's actual work

Beyond ritual, Mars responds to actual behaviour changes:

  • Regular physical exercise — channels Mars energy productively
  • Not skipping breakfast on Tuesdays (Mars fasting rules)
  • Consciously counting to 10 before arguing in marriage — the classic Mars-cooling instruction
  • Serving in physically-demanding causes — disaster relief, emergency service, medical field work
  • Avoiding red meat, alcohol, and stimulants when practicing intensive Mars remediation
On Red Coral (Moonga) — Mars's gemstone

Red Coral is Mars's classical gemstone. Like Rahu's hessonite and Saturn's blue sapphire, it CAN backfire when Mars is a functional malefic for your Ascendant. NEVER wear coral without proper chart analysis. When in doubt, use the practices above — they are safe universal remedies.

What Manglik remedies cannot do

  • They cannot guarantee marital happiness — that requires effort from both partners over years.
  • They cannot cancel an uncancelled Manglik overnight — remedies work gradually.
  • They cannot substitute for choosing a compatible partner. A good match matters more than any single remedy.

Reading Manglik remedies honestly

Manglik is over-diagnosed by most simple calculators (they ignore cancellation rules). Always verify with our Manglik checker which explicitly applies the classical cancellation rules before diagnosing. If your Manglik is genuinely present, Hanuman practice + honest matching remain the most consequential remedies. Skip the expensive pujas.

Sources and traditions

The Manglik rules themselves are not settled in the modern books we have checked. One author says the dosha is not found in the standard texts and rejects the usual cancellations; another lists a few antidotes (both partners Manglik, certain sign placements, Mars with Jupiter or the Moon) and adds that much importance need not be given to it. That is why this page asks you to check cancellation first instead of promising it. Hanuman, Rudra, Kartikeya and Narasimha as Mars's deities, toor daal as Mars's grain and the Mars seed mantra in its widely-accepted form follow one modern textbook's planet-by-planet remedy table. The rest of this page, including the Hanuman Chalisa and the other hymns and the Tuesday charity items beyond that grain, is devotional and family tradition that we have not traced to a classical text. Kumbh Vivah and Peepal Vivah in particular are folk-remedial practices; we have found no classical source for them.
