{# Language control. Caller provides `lang` (the RESOLVED language). ONE mechanism, deliberately — this file has now been wrong twice in opposite directions, and both failures came from the control navigating without persisting, or persisting without navigating. History, because it is the whole design rationale: v1 was a , because a programmatic form.submit() fires NO submit event and htmx never saw it. Turning the control into real buttons is what exposed it: a user-clicked submit DOES fire the event, htmx intercepts, and the attribute is the only thing that opts back out. Measured in Chromium, 9 switches (QA 2026-10-04): without it 6/9 left lang="en" and the root font-size at 16px instead of 17.28px; with it 0/9 stale. Causation isolated by toggling this one attribute. #}
