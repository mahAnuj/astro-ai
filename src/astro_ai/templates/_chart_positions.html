{# Compact positions line under a mini chart (#889). WHY THIS EXISTS. The diamond macro (`_diamond_labels.html`) drops a planet's degree in a cell too small for it (slot mode 1) and its retrograde flag in a triangle holding 7+ grahas (mode 2). On /kundli that is recoverable: the planetary-positions table sits under the chart. /matching has no such table, so a degree or an "R" dropped from a /matching chart was simply gone. This line is the table's stand-in there: EVERY planet of the chart, full degree and retrograde flag, whatever the diamond printed. Built from `ch.diamond_chart()` (the same data the diamond reads), flattened house by house in the order the chart gives.
