{# The short version — bold summary, sets expectations before the detail. #}

The short version

We keep your birth details so we can compute your chart. We keep your email only if you sign in with Google. We use standard third-party services for analytics, hosting, and AI-generated readings. We don't sell your data, we don't run ads, and we don't email you unless you subscribe. You can delete your data anytime by writing to us.

What we collect and why

Birth details (name, date, time, place)

Required to compute your Vedic birth chart. Stored in a signed browser cookie so you don't re-enter them each visit. If you sign in, they persist across your devices. Clear them from /account or by clearing your browser cookies.

Email + name (only if you sign in)

Used only to save your charts + reading history across devices. Google handles authentication; we never see your password. We request only your basic profile — no access to Gmail, Calendar, Drive, or Contacts.

Questions you ask

When you use the Ask feature, your question is sent (with your chart data) to an AI language-model provider to generate an answer. Cached briefly on our server so re-asking the same question is instant. Never used to train any AI model.

Anonymous usage patterns

We use standard third-party analytics to understand which features people actually use — page views, feature clicks, aggregate patterns. No birth details, no email, no question text is included. A tracker-blocking browser extension will opt you out; the site still works.

Cookies

One signed session cookie (your birth details, language preference, sign-in state). One small counter for the daily 3-free-questions Ask limit. A couple of analytics cookies set by our analytics provider. No advertising cookies. No cross-site tracking.

{# Third parties — high-level, no naming ceremony. #}

Who we share data with

To operate the site we rely on a small number of standard third-party services: cloud infrastructure (hosting + logs), AI language-model providers (chart-reading + Ask generation), Google Sign-In (if you sign in), and analytics (anonymous usage patterns). Each receives only what it needs; none are permitted to use your data to train models or serve you ads.

We do not sell your data to anyone. We do not run advertising. We do not share your personal details with astrologers, matrimonial services, or any other third party outside the operational vendors above.

{# Retention — plain list, no over-technical detail. #}

How long we keep it

  • • Browser cookies: extended on each visit; expire if you go inactive.
  • • Signed-in account data: until you request deletion.
  • • Cached AI-generated readings: a couple of weeks per unique chart+question combo.
  • • Operational logs: a few weeks for debugging, then discarded.
  • • Analytics data: the provider's default retention window.
{# Your rights — the important legal part. #}

Your rights

  • See your data. Visit /account when signed in — everything we hold on you is shown.
  • Correct your data. Re-enter your birth details from the home page or Kundli page to overwrite what we have.
  • Delete your data. Use the Feedback button in the bottom-right corner of any page, select "Delete my data", and we'll remove your record within 7 days. No questions asked.
  • Withdraw sign-in consent. Click "Sign out" in the nav. You can also revoke Shubh Bhagya's Google access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
{# Security + Children + Changes — compact, no visual weight competition. #}

Security

All traffic runs over HTTPS. Session data is cryptographically signed so it can't be tampered with. We take reasonable industry-standard precautions; no online service can guarantee absolute security, so treat your birth details as you would any personal record online.

Children

Shubh Bhagya isn't directed at children under 18. If a parent submits a child's birth details for a Kundli, that data is treated the same as adult data. Please don't have a child under 18 create an account.

Changes & contact

If we materially change this policy, we'll update the "Last updated" date at the top and — for signed-in users — surface a notice on /account. Minor edits happen silently.

Questions, requests, or concerns: use the Feedback button in the bottom-right corner of any page. A human replies, usually within 48 hours.

Written to be readable and honest. If anything reads ambiguously, please write to us and we'll clarify.
