Classical basis: Manglik Dosh rules are compiled from Jataka Parijata, Phaladeepika, Jataka Chandrika, and Muhurtha Chintamani (per P.V.R. Narasimha Rao's Jagannath Hora rule-set). The 6 qualifying houses (1, 2, 4, 7, 8, 12) are counted from three frames — Lagna, Moon and Venus — with severity graded 7th or 8th high, 1st, 4th or 12th moderate, 2nd alone low, and one grade lower when only the Venus frame qualifies. Cancellation rules that need only this chart — Cancer/Leo Lagna Yogakaraka exclusion, Mars in own sign or exaltation, Jupiter conjunction/aspect — are applied to this chart automatically; any that fire are listed above. Cancellation by matching with another Manglik needs both charts, so it is decided on the compatibility page rather than here, and only between partners of comparable severity: classical texts require comparability but do not define the band, so Bhagya treats equal or adjacent grades as comparable and a low-versus-high pairing as not comparable.
