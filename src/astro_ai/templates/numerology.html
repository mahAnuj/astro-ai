What numerology reads (and doesn't)

Numerology reads your name + birth date through a modern Western (Cheiro / Pythagorean) or ancient Chinese (Lo Shu) framework. Vedic astrology reads your full birth chart from a completely different tradition. These are separate systems, not two views of the same underlying method — sometimes their readings coincidentally align, sometimes they don't.

Numerology cannot predict marriage timing, career changes, or specific life events — those need chart-based Jyotish analysis. What numerology can offer: a compact prompt for self-reflection on your inner drive, external personality, and how you see your own life theme. Treat it as a mirror, not an oracle.

For a genuinely Vedic reading, generate your free Janma Kundli — that's classical Jyotish from the actual Sanskrit sources this site cites.

The 5 numbers we compute (and where they come from)

  • Life Path Number — birth-date reduction. Origin: L. Dow Balliett / Juno Jordan, USA, ~1908-1940s.
  • Destiny / Expression Number — English-letter name reduction. Origin: same Balliett/Jordan lineage.
  • Soul Urge Number — from name vowels. Origin: 20th-c. Western numerology.
  • Personality Number — from name consonants. Origin: 20th-c. Western numerology.
  • Name Number — Chaldean total of your name letters. Origin: Cheiro (William John Warner, 1866-1936); tables in wide Indian circulation trace to him.
