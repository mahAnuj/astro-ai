What numerology reads

Numerology reads your name + birth date. Vedic astrology reads your full birth chart. These are different lenses on the same person — sometimes they agree, sometimes they don't. When both point the same way, the signal is stronger.

Numerology cannot predict marriage timing, career changes, or specific life events — those need chart-based analysis. What numerology offers well: a compact snapshot of your inner drive, external personality, and life theme.

For deeper analysis, generate your free Janma Kundli and compare.

The 5 numbers we compute

  • Life Path Number — from your birth date. The main theme of your life journey.
  • Destiny (Expression) Number — from your full name. Your natural role in the world.
  • Soul Urge Number — from the vowels of your name. What you inwardly want.
  • Personality Number — from the consonants. How others perceive you.
  • Name Number — Chaldean total of your name. The vibration your name carries.
