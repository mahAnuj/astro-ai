Kaal Sarp Yoga does NOT appear in Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra / Brihat Jataka / Saravali / Phaladeepika / Jataka Parijata / Uttara Kalamrita. Its first verifiable Indian print discussion is in The Astrological Magazine, late 1970s–early 1980s. K.N. Rao publicly called it "one of the biggest frauds in astrology." B.V. Raman added it as an APPENDIX to the 9th edition of his book, because it had "become much feared" — his treatment was nuanced. #}
