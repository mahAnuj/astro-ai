What your Kundali looks like

Sample chart shown — yours will be computed from your birth details

Twelve houses (Bhavas) arranged around a central diamond. Each house holds a Rashi (sign) and any planets sitting in it.

  • · Numbers show the Rashi (Aries=1, Taurus=2 … Pisces=12).
  • · Top centre is your Lagna (Ascendant) — the rising sign at your birth.
  • · Planet codes (Su, Mo, Ma, Me, Ju, Ve, Sa, Ra, Ke) appear inside the house they occupy, with their degree.
