1. What Shubh Bhagya is

Shubh Bhagya (shubhagya.com) is a free web tool that computes Vedic (sidereal Lahiri) birth charts using the Swiss Ephemeris library and generates natural-language readings using third-party large language models (LLMs) applied to the classical Jyotish rules encoded in our software. It's built and operated by an individual (not an incorporated company), currently based in India.

2. Astrology disclaimer

Astrology in the classical Vedic tradition is treated as a lens for self-understanding and timing, not as a deterministic prediction of future events. The readings you generate on Shubh Bhagya reflect that same philosophy — they describe tendencies, potentials, and periods, not fixed outcomes. Even the most classical astrologer will tell you that free will and karma-in-motion always modulate what a chart shows.

Specifically, do NOT use Shubh Bhagya as a source for:

  • Medical decisions — the "Health" section of any reading is not a diagnosis. Consult a licensed physician.
  • Legal decisions — consult a licensed lawyer.
  • Financial or investment decisions — consult a SEBI-registered advisor (India) or your local licensed equivalent.
  • Marriage-go / no-go verdicts — Ashtakoot Guna Milan is a starting-point filter, not the final answer. A trained astrologer looking at both full charts + the 7th house lord + Venus + Upapada + Dashas is a different-quality reading than any automated tool.
  • Predictions about death, terminal illness, or similar high-stakes outcomes — the /ask tool refuses these questions, and classical astrology traditionally does not answer them without extreme caution.

3. AI-generated content disclaimer

Chart-reading narratives (the natural-language sections on /kundli, /matching, /numerology, /ask, etc.) are produced by AI language models operating on your chart's computed placements. AI language models can and do produce plausible-sounding text that is inaccurate or generic. A trained human astrologer's intuition catches patterns that AI cannot — as one of our advisors put it, AI has "no connection with paramatma." We disclose this on the /ask page itself and repeat it here.

The underlying chart computations (planetary positions, dashas, yogas, Manglik detection, Sade Sati phase, Ashtakoot scores, Kaal Sarp geometry, etc.) are deterministic and sourced from the Swiss Ephemeris — these are as accurate as the astronomical algorithms allow (typically arc-second precision). The narrative interpretation layer that turns those computations into English/Hindi prose is AI-assisted and should be read with that in mind.
