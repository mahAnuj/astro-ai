What Shubh Bhagya is

Shubh Bhagya (shubhagya.com) is a free web tool that computes Vedic (sidereal Lahiri) birth charts using standard astronomical libraries, and generates natural-language readings using AI language models applied to classical Jyotish rules. Built and operated by an individual based in India.

{# Astrology disclaimer — the most important part. #}

Astrology as guidance, not prediction

Classical Vedic astrology is a lens for self-understanding and timing — not a deterministic prediction of future events. Readings on Shubh Bhagya describe tendencies, potentials, and periods, not fixed outcomes. Even the most classical astrologer will tell you that free will and karma-in-motion modulate what any chart shows.

Do not use Shubh Bhagya as a substitute for:

  • • Medical decisions — the health section of a reading is not a diagnosis. Consult a licensed physician.
  • • Legal decisions — consult a licensed lawyer.
  • • Financial decisions — consult a registered financial advisor.
  • • Marriage go/no-go verdicts — Ashtakoot matching is a starting-point filter, not a final answer. A trained astrologer looking at both full charts is a different-quality reading than any automated tool.
  • • High-stakes predictions — questions about death, terminal illness, or similar are refused by the Ask tool by design.
{# AI-generated content disclaimer. #}

AI-generated content

The natural-language sections of readings (on Kundli, Matching, Numerology, Ask, etc.) are produced by AI language models operating on your chart's computed placements. AI can produce plausible-sounding text that is sometimes inaccurate or generic. A trained human astrologer's intuition catches patterns AI cannot.

The underlying chart computations (planetary positions, dashas, yogas, Manglik detection, Sade Sati phase, matching scores, etc.) are deterministic and astronomically precise. The narrative interpretation layer that turns those numbers into prose is AI-assisted and should be read with that in mind.
