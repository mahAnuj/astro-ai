# The request-indexing list asks first for a not-indexed next-year /learn page

- **Date:** 2026-11-28
- **PR / issue:** #1571

## Why, measured

`rank_requests` in `scripts/fetch_index_status.py` ranks by impressions, so a brand-new
page that Google has not indexed (0 impressions) sank below every stale page and was never
shown to the owner. In `measurements/index-status.json` (2026-11-28T03:26Z) the not-indexed
`/learn/gochar-2027-...` was in no top-10 slot.

**The rule.** A row that is `not_indexed` AND whose `/learn/<slug>` (or `/hi/learn/<slug>`)
slug names next year (`today.year + 1`, digit-bounded) is listed first, with the extra
reason `names <year>: ask before it has impressions`. Blocked, canonical-mismatch and
not-inspected rows are still skipped; a stale-only row never qualifies. The year follows the
date, so on 2027-01-01 it selects 2028 pages (none exist) and the list reverts to plain
impression order.

**Scope is `/learn` only.** A bare "slug contains 2027" rule would match 26
`/panchang/2027-01-01..26` daily pages, 9 of them not indexed, and fill the whole list with
date pages. With the `/learn` scope the committed capture yields exactly three pages:
gochar-2027, vivah-muhurat-2027 and griha-pravesh-muhurat-2026-2027 (a range, but it names
2027 and is forward-looking; asked for on purpose).

**Capped at `limit // 2` (5 of 10).** A year-end batch of new pages must not take all ten
slots and hide every stale page. No new constant.

**Rejected.**
- A separate `must_request` key: needs `ops_data.py` and `admin_ops.html` to change too, and
  makes two sources of "what to request".
- Hardcoding 2027: rots on 2027-01-01.
- Slug-contains-year on every path: the 26 panchang pages above.
- A predecessor-to-successor map (the issue's wider form): no source of truth exists
  (`learn_registry` has no successor field) and it would be a second table.
- A blended impressions score: a 0-impression page cannot win without an arbitrary constant.
- Hand-editing `measurements/index-status.json`: it is generated by the collector.
- Widening `limit`: `INDEX_LIST_MAX = 10` in `ops_data` caps the panel at 10 anyway.

Cost: 0 LLM tokens; 0 extra inspection calls (214 of 2,000/day, unchanged).
