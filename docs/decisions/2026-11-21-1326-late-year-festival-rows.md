# The registry gains five late-year festival rows, filling the 2026-11-22..2027-01-14 hole

- **Date:** 2026-11-21
- **PR / issue:** #1326

## Why, measured

At `d55534c` the registry held 35 festivals and not one date fell between 2026-11-22 and
2027-01-14 (the issue's repro prints `[]`). Nothing in the registry header, the `Festival`
docstring or `tests/festival_dates_expected.json` excludes those months, so `astro-auditor`
ruled it **missing data, not deliberate scope**. It is now 40 festivals, and the window holds
2026-11-24, 12-01, 12-14, 12-20 and 12-23.

## The five rows

| slug | rule | 2026 | 2027 | 2028 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `kartika-purnima` | Kartika Shukla Purnima | 2026-11-24 | 2027-11-14 | 2028-11-02 |
| `kalabhairav-jayanti` | Margashirsha Krishna Ashtami (purnimanta) = Kartika Krishna Ashtami (amanta) | 2026-12-01 | 2027-11-20 | 2028-11-09 |
| `vivah-panchami` | Margashirsha Shukla Panchami | 2026-12-14 | 2027-12-03 | 2028-11-21 |
| `gita-jayanti` (= Mokshada Ekadashi, one row) | Margashirsha Shukla Ekadashi | 2026-12-20 | 2027-12-09 | 2028-11-27 |
| `dattatreya-jayanti` (= Margashirsha Purnima, one row) | Margashirsha Purnima | 2026-12-23 | 2027-12-13 | 2028-12-01 |

Each date was read off Drik Panchang (New Delhi, IST): the year page and the monthly page
agreed. An independent Swiss Ephemeris computation (Lahiri, Delhi) agrees on all 15
festival-years, and the repo's own `lunar_observances` engine agrees on the 9 Purnima and
Ekadashi dates. The dates are **typed**, not generated by `lunar_dates.shukla_tithi_date` or
`sankranti.sankranti_date`, so `tests/festival_dates_expected.json` (also typed) is a second
transcription for Gate 1 rather than a copy. No new date collides with an existing one, so
Gate 5's shared-date table is unchanged. Nine new `tithi_interval_invariants` tie the rows to
Devuthani Ekadashi, Tulsi Vivah and Diwali, which were verified earlier and independently.

`dattatreya-jayanti` 2026: Purnima is a kshaya tithi (12-23 10:47 to 12-24 06:58 IST, no
sunrise inside it), so sunrise cannot place it and pradosh on 12-23 fixes the day.

## Dropped, with the reason

- **Utpanna Ekadashi** - 2028: Drik's primary date (11-12) and the repo sunrise rule
  disagree by a 19-33 second knife-edge. The sources conflict, so it does not ship.
- **Dev Deepawali, Guru Nanak Jayanti, Lohri, Vaikuntha Chaturdashi** - Drik prints no date,
  so there is no second source. (Dev Deepawali is also the evening BEFORE Kartika Purnima's
  Drik date in 2027 and 2028, so the Kartika Purnima copy does not say "the evening of this
  date".)
- **Saphala / Putrada Ekadashi** - outside the inclusion rule: only the two Chaturmas
  Ekadashis are tabulated; the monthly ones are the computed layer.
- **Dhanu Sankranti** - the only candidate where sources conflict
  (`sankranti.sankranti_date(2028, 'Dhanu')` = 12-16, Drik prints 12-15), with no Gate 2 or
  3 coverage; it would only shrink a 33-day gap to 30, no worse than gaps already in the
  file. A separate PR if wanted.
- Christmas is not a Hindu festival.

## Latent defects found, not fixed here

Neither is called by these rows (the dates are typed), so both are filed separately:

1. `lunar_dates.shukla_tithi_date` mis-resolves Margashirsha 2027 (returns the 2026 month).
2. `lunar_observances` emits Drik's *Gauna* Ekadashi date for 2027-01 and 2028-11, where
   Drik's primary date is a day earlier.

## Grounding

No `knowledge/*.md` source covers the customs of these festivals, and `data/` was absent in
this checkout, so the corpus was not searched. The copy is therefore plain description of
custom ("is observed by", "tradition holds"): no astrological claim, no Kharmas, and no
wedding-muhurat claim on Vivah Panchami. The dates rest on the almanac and the ephemeris,
not on the corpus.

## Cost

Zero tokens on a non-festival day. On each of the five new days the rashifal prompt gains
the one festival signal line (`llm._build_rashifal_day_signals`: "Today is <name> - <note>
Weave this in if it fits."), measured with tiktoken `o200k_base`: 0 tokens before; after,
en 40 / 40 / 46 / 56 / 55 and hi 45 / 45 / 46 / 61 / 57 (Kartika Purnima, Kalabhairav,
Vivah Panchami, Gita, Dattatreya). The largest is 61, under 1% of the 8,000 TPM per-model
ceiling; about 1.2k tokens per festival day if all 24 rashifal calls carry it, five days a
year. `verify_token_budget.py` pins its clock to 2026-10-17, a day with no festival, so its
figures do not move. No LLM call is added.
