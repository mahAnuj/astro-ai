# The rashifal may not invent the reader's life: opening moves and shapes start from the day, the prompt forbids invented people and events, and the gate refuses a named relative or boss

- **Date:** 2026-11-09
- **PR / issue:** #815

## Why, measured

The owner's Aries email opened "You left the kitchen conversation with your sister hanging" and read as fiction; the live pages the same week carried "your mother's kitchen" (Cancer), "Item four on your list" (Virgo), an invented event (Leo, "Someone notices what you did last week") and invented dialogue (Libra). Root cause in `llm.py`: five of the eleven `_OPENING_MOVES` told the model to assert a fact about the reader ("something that happened yesterday and is not finished", "a person they have not replied to", "an object in the room with them", "a number ... that matters to them", "a decision they have deliberately not made"), two `_RASHIFAL_SHAPES` said "open mid-situation" and "someone the reader has been avoiding", and the prompt asked for "concrete images" with nothing forbidding invention. Fix: the eleven moves now start from the day (weekday, tithi, nakshatra, element vs day-lord, a question asked and never answered for them), pool sizes kept at 7 and 11; `rashifal_single.txt` gains NEVER INVENT THE READER'S LIFE (no specific people, no invented events, dialogue, objects or history, conditionals instead of assertions, every paragraph from a signal in the user message), and its two examples that themselves asserted reader facts are rewritten; `rashifal_quality.STYLE_RULES` gains "invents the reader's people" ("your" + a named relation or a boss; "your family" and "people you work with" pass), which the nightly job's own dry-run gate makes it fix before publishing. Token cost measured with `verify_token_budget.py`: 2,927 -> 3,017 per call (en, +90, inside the 5% allowance); a first draft at +395 failed the gate and was cut to rules only, the rationale moved to code. Invented events and dialogue are not regex-shaped, so the prompt carries those. **Then, same day (owner: "Don't map any fixed lines for the opening. Try to use simple language that masses in India can understand easily"):** `_OPENING_MOVES` and every rashi's `opening_style` are removed, so the model opens in its own words; the banned openings, "do not open two readings the same way" and the rotating shape still work against convergence (#513's measured problem). The publish gate's `opening_repeats` cross-day check does NOT refuse a repeat: it is logged, not fatal, and English only, because its firing rate is unmeasured. So this protection is weaker than before; QA flagged the earlier wording of this sentence as false. The LANGUAGE section now asks for Class-8 words, 8-15 word sentences, no idioms or metaphors (with the measured offenders as examples), and everyday spoken Hindi. **And the nightly brief was blind to the gochara:** `scripts/rashifal_brief.py` called `llm._rashifal_user_message`, which never existed, and fell back to a hand copy from before #513/#523, so every Claude-written reading lacked TODAY'S GOCHARA, the per-area counts and the shape. It now calls `_format_rashifal_message` with the same per-rashi gochara and shape as `generate_rashifal`, and `tests/test_rashifal_brief.py` (which the old docstring cited and which did not exist) pins all twelve messages to the live builder. Tokens: system prompt 2,927 -> 3,017 (+90, at the 5% allowance), full call 4,186 -> 4,193 (+7: the opening hints that left the user message paid for the new rules).
