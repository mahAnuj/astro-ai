# The rashifal is told only what the corpus says, and a gate checks its tone against the evidence

- **Date:** 2026-11-09
- **PR / issue:** #815

## Why, measured

Owner: "ensure nothing is cooked up but grounded in astrology rules." An audit of every signal the reading receives against `knowledge/*.md` found: (1) the retrograde line said significations "feel internal/reflective/second-look", while grahas.md §13 states the classical rule is the opposite, a retrograde planet is STRONGER (the weakened reading is Western), a direct contradiction; (2) the prompt's weekday table carried qualities the corpus never gives the planet (Moon "rest", Mars "competition, disputes", Jupiter "expansion, generosity", Saturn "structure, reflection"); (3) popular tithi characters ("quiet resets", "Ganesh energy") reached the prose through `panchang.tithi_significance`, labelled in the prompt as popular but still used; (4) element and quality lines paraphrased Raman loosely; (5) "a Shiva festival lands harder for a Water sign" had no source. Now the day signals carry only corpus lines: the day lord's significations verbatim from grahas.md (`llm._DAY_LORD_SIGNIFIES`, pinned to the table by a test), Raman's avoid-list caution computed per tithi (4th/6th/8th/12th/14th, Purnima, Amavasya; `_tithi_is_raman_avoid`) and no other tithi character, the nakshatra named with no qualities, retrogrades as grahas.md §13 states them; the prompt quotes Raman's element and quality words and tells the model to add no quality a signal was not given. `rashifal_quality.tone_conflicts` refuses a line with no supporting planet that sounds positive, a fully supported line that warns, and a day with at most one favourable graha of nine called good; `publish_rashifal._todays_evidence` computes the same evidence the brief hands the writer. Mixed evidence is left to the writer; the lexicons are English. The prompt got 10% smaller (3,160 -> 2,837 tokens): the corpus lines are shorter than the popular tables were.
